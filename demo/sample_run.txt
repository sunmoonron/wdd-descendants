── 6. the same items, with the standard tools ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Directional ablation (Arditi et al. 2024): remove the mean female-minus-male direction from every
  block output 0..L at every token. A steering vector: add the male-minus-female mean state at the
  most consistent block, at every token, scaled.
    method                                     female→male   male→female   wikitext KL
    negate 8 rows (in the weights)                   58.6%          5.2%        0.0052
    ablate the direction, every block                89.7%          0.0%        0.0097
    steering vector x1 at block 6                    98.3%          0.0%        0.1157
    steering vector x2 at block 6                   100.0%          0.0%        0.7480
    steering vector x4 at block 6                    37.9%         62.1%        2.9020
  Read it as a trade-off: the row edit moves 58.6% at KL 0.0052; ablating the direction moves 89.7%
  at 1.9x that KL; the first steering scale that matches the ablation (steering vector x1) costs 22x
  the row edit's KL; at the largest scale the steering vector breaks the model.

  ACT 3  tamper with one row, then audit the model blind
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  Pythia-410m, block 4 (4,096 MLP rows). Someone edits one write row in place. The auditor gets only
  the tampered weights and 16 ordinary wikitext windows: no original checkpoint, no diff. Three
  detectors (e623, e648b, e651): the column's norm (z-score > 3), the read-write cosine (above the
  block's 99th percentile; in GELU models most rows sit well below zero), and activation consistency
  (does the write agree with the states where its neuron fires, minus its own write; below the 1st
  percentile).
  loaded EleutherAI/pythia-410m (59s)
  block 4 unedited: read-write cosine median -0.28 (76% of rows below -0.1). The tamper targets come
  from the 40 most-used rows (the top 1% by mean |activation|) (61s)

  tampering -0.69, consistency +0.09.
