# 08. The causal coordinate: compression, kill-tree, P_l and the quotient picture (S22-S30)

**Question.** Once a write has spread into a full-dimensional descendant, how many directions carry its causal content, what are they, and what do they buy?
**Established.** A descendant needs 512-1024 directions to reconstruct, but its identity, next state, logit footprint and KL each need 8-32 (e286), for every perturbation family, on or off the natural manifold (e304, e310). They are the high-variance part of the perturbation cloud, not a spectral subspace of transport or read-out (e288, e305, e308, e309), carried almost unchanged by transport, with their future under intervention predicted at 0.95-0.99 (e293, e307, e315). Steering is selective but incomplete (e289, e292) and the WDD ledger predicts joint coordinates at 0.30-0.68 (e313), yet the application failed: no functional units, no substitution, no attribution gain (e316, e316b). Session 31 found that shuffled-target fits, PCA and the discarded complement decode as well (e340, e346, e347), so the 16-dimensional core is one of many equivalent high-variance projections, not a privileged subspace.
**Start here:** e286, e288, e305, e310, e313 · **Sessions:** S22-S30 · **Scripts:** `scripts/e286_*.py` to `scripts/e316b_*.py`, one per id

## How the results flow

- `e286 → e287 → e288 → e340`: two rungs (8-32 vs 512-1024); e287 placed the read-out in the centroid span; e288's matched controls moved it to the cloud's high-variance part, only identity being span-specific; S31 found shuffled-target bases, PCA and the core's complement as informative as the supervised core (e340, e346, e347).
- `e289 → e292 → e302 → e315 → e348`: steering is selective but incomplete; coordinates are linear channels; the state's future is predicted without refitting, the logits only partly; cancelling the core restores 1-6% of a logit deviation.
- `e293 → e296 → e307 → e320`: near-diagonal transport; coordinates persist two blocks on, then block to block; e320-e321 keep whole-depth persistence only in a fixed basis and in GPT-2, Pythia, OLMo.
- `e290 → e301 → e331 → e432`: the massive-neuron prediction failed and became a set point unique to SmolLM2's massive neuron; e331 sees it in OLMo at 4x; e432 rejects it as an account of M.
- `e304 → e306 → e310 → e311 → e312`: the ladder is generic; families converge on one coordinate system with depth; off-manifold perturbations get the same funnel; the map is many-to-one and transfers near the output. S31: no family breaks it (e342), no cosets or fibre structure (e336, e338).
- `e305 → e308 → e309`: P_l is no operator, read-out, sensitivity or state-variance subspace; it was read as the cloud's dominant-variance subspace (H112), and after S31 as any such projection (THEORY 3h).
- `e300 → e303 → e313 → e316 → e316b`: cores share a 6-8-dimensional space; a write predicts its own core in GPT-2, Pythia, OLMo; the ledger predicts joint coordinates (z = Zc); the atlas fails as an application; e332: atoms are no privileged basis.
