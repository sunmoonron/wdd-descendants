# 57. Calendar rings and their parameters (S129)

**Question.** Block-sparse featurizers treat a representation as a sparse sum of low-dimensional manifolds carried by learned blocks. The calendar rings of Engels et al. 2024 are the best-known case. In the model's own parameters, where does such a ring live, who writes it, and what happens to it through depth?

**Established.**
- On six models the seven weekday means at the best block lie in cyclic order in a plane (7 of 7 adjacencies, shuffle p at most 0.01) and so do the twelve months (12 of 12), the replication of Engels et al. 2024; the token embeddings already carry the weekday order in all six models (7 of 7) and the month order in three; the blocks do not keep the embedding's ring but build a new one in a nearly orthogonal plane: the ring pattern's energy in the embedding's plane is 0.00 to 0.11 by block 2 and the best block's plane has principal cosines of at most 0.29 with it (e633).
- The new ring is written by the MLP rows: their writes carry 0.69 to 1.00 of the weekday pattern and 0.80 to 1.02 of the month pattern at the best block, the rest attention; it is diffuse in most models (Pythia-160m 181, GPT-2 small 156, Qwen2.5-0.5B 68, Pythia-410m 394, SmolLM2-135M 130, OLMo-1B 28 rows for half of the weekday pattern; months 252, 182, 23, 522, 169, 4), with OLMo-1B's months carried half by 4 rows, the one small native block; the most day-selective rows write at the day's angle in four of the six models (phase coding Pythia-160m -0.04, GPT-2 small 0.36, Qwen2.5-0.5B 0.34, Pythia-410m -0.03, SmolLM2-135M 0.28, OLMo-1B 0.17 against random rows near zero), absent in both Pythias (e633).
- The ring's radius relative to the state norm falls with depth, from 0.33 to 0.55 at the embedding to 0.10 to 0.27 at the last block before the final norm, while the cyclic order persists on the best block's plane through the later blocks in every model but Pythia-160m's months, which lose it after block 10 (e633).
- Reading: the calendar manifold is known; what is new and small is where it lives and who writes it: the embedding's ring is abandoned by block 2, the blocks rebuild one in a new plane, the MLP rows write it, diffusely in five models and in a handful of rows in one, and in four models the rows that select a day also point at it. A descriptive result with a null and six models, not a nothingburger and not a paper; the one claim it supports for the program is that a manifold's chart belongs to a coalition, which is what the record said of directions.

**Session 130 (the edges).** Phase coding survives a label-shuffle null, a template split and a row-count sweep in every sequential model, sharpens with scale (GPT-2 small to large 0.36 to 0.70, OPT-125m to 1.3b 0.58 to 0.76) and sits at the null in the three Pythias (-0.04, -0.03, -0.02 for days). The phase-coded rows are block-0 token detectors (0.71 to 1.00 of the selective rows in block 0 in the small models), their coding highest at block 0 and falling with depth, so the ring's plane is laid down by block 0; the Pythias' block 0 amplifies the embedding's ring instead (+1.48 to +1.89) and does not phase-code. The embedding's ring is carried and amplified in place (energy at block 2 of 1.02 to 10.14 times the embedding's own at cosines 0.92 to 1.00 in the Pythias, GPT-2 small, OPT-125m and OLMo-1B), not abandoned, and reversed in the Qwens' months (cosine -0.16 at block 2, -0.76 by block 8). The writers for half of the MLP ring pattern are 4 to 522 rows, nearly disjoint from the detectors (overlap at most 0.22), and take 0.33 to 0.53 of the refit radius in GPT-2 small, Qwen and SmolLM2-135M days (OLMo-1B days 0.07) where the detectors, top-norm rows and random rows take under 0.15; the remaining rows recover at most a quarter of the loss. Removing the writers lowers calendar arithmetic with the dose at near-zero general loss in GPT-2 small months (0.71 to 0.31 at half, 0.08 at 90 percent), SmolLM2-135M days (0.71 to 0.25), Qwen months (0.92 to 0.44 at 90 percent) and OPT-125m, and not in OLMo-1B months (1.00 throughout) or Pythia-410m days; the detectors and random rows change it little, and e637's activation-matched control hurt it only through general damage. The writers' effect on the arithmetic survives a quantile-matched control in 5 of 18 model-calendar pairs (GPT-2 small months, GPT-2 medium days, GPT-2 large months, SmolLM2-135M days, SmolLM2-360M days) and not in OPT-125m months, SmolLM2-135M months; the other calendar's writers hurt the task only in SmolLM. Reading: what holds at the edges: a ledger-attributed set of ring writers, distinct from the token detectors that phase-code, is a small, cheap-to-remove, dose-dependent causal substrate of calendar arithmetic that survives a quantile-matched control in the GPT-2 family (small months, medium days, large months, where the writers also cut the reverse task) and in SmolLM2 days (135M and 360M); in OPT-125m months the matched rows hurt the task too, and the writers do not move the arithmetic of OPT-1.3b, Qwen2.5-0.5B, OLMo-1B or Pythia-410m although they halve its ring, so the ring is the arithmetic's substrate in some models and decoration in others; the phase coding itself is a block-0 property of sequential models that sharpens with scale and is absent in the parallel-residual Pythias; and the embedding's ring is carried, not abandoned, and reversed in the Qwens' months..

**Session 131 (the rotation).** Rotating the writers' in-plane write components by one class step turns the ring by 0.6 to 1.6 steps in every pair at no cost in loss, while matched rows barely move it; the answers follow only in part, 0.11 to 0.25 of them moving two steps ahead in GPT-2 medium days, SmolLM2-135M days, SmolLM2-360M days and almost none elsewhere.

## How the results flow

- `e638 → e639`: the matched control left the writers causal; the rotation asks whether the phase they write is what the arithmetic reads. It is not.
- `e633 → e634 → e635 → e636 → e637 → e638`: each experiment attacks an edge of the previous one: the null and the split, the carrier, the location, the specificity, the matched control.

- `e545, e546 → e633`: the coalition that builds a word's extreme, asked of a manifold instead of a direction.
- `e470 → e633`: the band in which a variable is written and re-written, seen for a ring: the embedding's plane fades and the blocks build another.
