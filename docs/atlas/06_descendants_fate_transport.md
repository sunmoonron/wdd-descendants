# 06. Descendants I: a write's fate and its transport (S4-S11)

**Question.** Where does a write go once its atom is no longer readable, and how does its identity travel downstream?
**Established.** A write is scattered, not damped: its footprint keeps or grows its energy while its own direction decays, and every unit is accounted for by thousands of later MLP writers plus attention, without collisions or funnelling of real writes (e221-e225). The descendant still names its source neuron in 88-98% of tokens (e227) and is mostly the transported image of the write vector: predictable zero-shot, independent of magnitude, compositional, robust to weight noise and portable (e231, e233, e234, e241, e242). Transport keeps the angles of injected pairs (e238; gain concentration, not isometry, per e282 and e285), carries 6.0-6.4 of 7 bits to six blocks (e237), and moves vectors, not neuron identities (e245, e248). It is a classification coordinate, not a decomposition basis: a transported dictionary fails in 5/5 (e236), and the native chart misses injected writes by prominence (e247); massive-activation neurons, transported normally but with natural descendants opposing their transplant images, are the boundary (e250). (+k: k blocks after the write; mid: the mid layer.)
**Start here:** e222, e223, e227, e231, e236 · **Sessions:** S4-S11 · **Scripts:** `scripts/e221_perturbation_fate.py` to `scripts/e250_massive_boundary.py`, helpers `scripts/desc_common.py`

## How the results flow

- `e215 → e221 → e222 → e223`: e221 tests e215's survival floor and finds energy kept while direction scatters; e222 confirms it with real writes; e223 accounts for the footprint exactly.
- `e223 → e224 → e225 → e229 → e243`: the footprint neither merges (e224) nor funnels (e225), unlike random perturbations; dispersal is learned (e229), and e243 orders the training phases, with late-block scrambling last.
- `e227 → e230 → e232 → e237 → e252`: footprint identity (e227) becomes a state-level dictionary (e230), a depth crossover (e232; its depth follows e215's gains in Pythia and Qwen, within a level in OLMo) and a 128-source capacity (e237); e252 narrows the late loss to 3/5.
- `e227 → e231 → e233 → e234 → e235 → e239`: session 7 attacks the signature: it is mostly the vector's image (e231) and survives weight noise, a corpus change, path freezing and a checkpoint swap.
- `e231 → e236 → e240 → e277`: a transported dictionary should then read states at depth; it fails in 5/5 (e236), e240 blames blur rather than coherence, and e277's small coherent fraction explains why.
- `e231 → e238 → e252 → e285`: angle preservation (e238) holds to the last block (e252) and is reread as gain concentration (e282, e285).
- `e226 → e241 → e245 → e247 → e248 → e275b`: near-linearity gives a compositional code (e241; e285b finds many writes at least as linear as one); transport finds the largest of 64 writes where native reading fails and treats random vectors like writes (e245); the native miss is prominence (e247); e248's operator reads a+b as a+b but aligns weakly with natural descendants (e275b). e246 builds e242's images from random tokens.
- `e242 → e244 → e250 → e257 → e284b → e290`: massive neurons fail zero-shot (e242), not by gating (e244), yet transport normally (e250); the sink route was killed (e257), and SmolLM2's block-10 MLP answers removal as a set point (e284b, e290).
