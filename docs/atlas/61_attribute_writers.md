# 61. Who writes an attribute, and do its writers transfer (S146)

**Question.** Sessions 144 and 145 found that negating the rows of an attribute's relay chain, found by the WDD vote, flips the attribute where a naive activation difference fails. Is the vote a better localizer than the field's tools, can splitting it by pole give two-way edits, and do the rows transfer to models trained much further?

**Established.**
- Against the field's standard localizers the WDD vote is not special: averaged over doses, attribution patching comes within 0.05 of the vote or beats it in 14 of 16 model-attribute cells (behind it by more than 0.05 only for gender in the 1.5B models, 0.42 against 0.47, 0.46 against 0.56), the edit-specific gradient attribution in 14 (its first-order estimate failing for gender in the 1.5B models, 0.16, 0.13), and causal block patching within 0.1 in 13, while the naive activation difference trails the vote by 0.04 to 0.59 and random rows flip at most 0.02; patching ranks block 0 first for almost every attribute and model, the block whose MLP output carries the word itself (e664).
- A plain direct-contribution ranking with no pursuit and no dictionary (each row's activation change times its write's alignment with the pole difference at its own block) comes within 0.05 of the vote or beats it in 16 of 16 cells (mean over doses, direct minus vote, -0.05 to +0.13), and the vote's top 32 rows sit earlier than the direct ranking's (mean block 3.2 to 6.1 against 4.1 to 8.1), as the cumulative dictionary's eligibility of early rows at every later level would make them (e664b).
- The vote order's top 32 rows do mix rows more active on each pole (10 to 14 of 32 more active on male items for gender, 7 to 10 on singular for number), and the unmarked pole's rows push along the pole difference too (0.82 to 1.00 of gender's), as Gemini said; but negating them does not set the marked pole: male to female 0.03 to 0.31 (0.31 only in Qwen2.5-0.5B), singular to plural 0.00, the number rows still flipping plural to singular (0.06 to 0.30); negating the marked pole's rows strips the mark cleanly in the 1.5B models (female to male 0.82 with male 0.00 to 0.02) but also flips male items in the 0.5B models (0.16 to 0.17), so e662's high-dose male flips are not only the unmarked rows' doing; and the four target-quadrant edits put 0.48 to 0.57 of the kinship items on king, 0.31 to 0.44 on prince, 0.28 to 0.39 on queen and 0.13 to 0.26 on princess, a quarter being already there (e663).
- The base model's rows edit heavily continued-pretrained derivatives zero-shot: on Qwen2.5-Coder-0.5B and Qwen2.5-Coder-0.5B-Instruct, whose MLP write rows sit at median cosine 0.63 and 0.44 with the base (e619 found 0.997 after instruct tuning), negating the base model's WDD chain flips female items 0.68 and 0.56 against 0.59 and 0.40 for the derivative's own chain, and plurals 0.98 and 0.93 against 0.98 and 0.80, with random chains at most 0.02; the number chain's rows drifted much less than the rest (cosine with the base 0.83 and 0.76), but the gender chain's rows in the instruct model drifted about as much as an average row (0.46) and still carry the edit; the own chains overlap the base's in Jaccard 0.20 to 0.57 (e665).
- Reading: checked against the literature and these runs, Gemini's six claims sort into three kinds. Its mechanism for e662's high-dose flips is partly right (the vote mixes rows active on each pole) but its payoff, two-way surgery and four-quadrant composition, fails. Its 'echo chamber' is real only for the naive baseline: attribution patching, causal patching and a plain direct-contribution ranking all localize the writers as well as the vote, so e662 did not show WDD beating the field's tools. And its 'write once, patch everywhere' holds further than expected, through a code continuation whose rows moved far, where the literature had no data; together with the markedness result, prior art exists for every claim in some form (Jumelet et al. 2019 for the default classes, Arditi et al. 2024 for ablation over addition, MEMIT for spreading an edit over layers), and what remains new is narrow.
