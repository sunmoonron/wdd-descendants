# 30. The tails: what the cloud's persistence is, what a Gaussian cloud cannot give, the sparse row-wise extremes that carry the native fifth, and who writes them (S92-S94)

**Question.** Sessions 90-91 found the one-way gate to be the persistence of the native maximum and that persistence to be shared by any direction drawn with the state cloud's covariance. What in the cloud persists; does a Gaussian cloud with the real covariance and the real temporal dynamics generate the native vocabulary; and if not, where in the real cloud is the native fifth, and what of it does the vocabulary need?

**Established.**
- The cloud persists in most of its directions (277 above 0.9 across a thousand steps, ranks 257-512 at 0.85), its dynamic modes are its variance modes (top-32 overlap 0.95), the subspace rotates slowly and the rows do not lead it; a native word's whole projection profile persists no more than an isotropic atom's (0.905 against 0.899) but its extremes persist beyond its bulk (conditional persistence 0.900 in the bulk, 0.961 at 1.2-1.5) where an isotropic atom's regress like its bulk (0.900, 0.901) (e542).
- A Gaussian cloud with the real covariance at every checkpoint cannot give the native vocabulary at any temporal dynamics: independent samples keep the criterion (0.88) and much of the retention (0.37) with no per-position persistence (the spatial part of wordhood); coupled clouds restore the per-position persistence and the peak's provenance; every one gives words at S 1.08 with 125-144 entries per interval and retention 0.37-0.42 against 1.28, 96 and 0.50 for the real states; the native fifth lives in the non-Gaussian extremes (e543).
- The native fifth is in the top of each position's profile, and sparse: the top four real projections at each position (four rows of 53,248) grafted onto the coupled Gaussian cloud restore the plateau (1.32 against 1.28 real, 1.07 Gaussian), the turnover (91 entries per interval against 96 and 135), the retention (0.51 against 0.50 and 0.35) and the gate (0.88 against 0.79 and 0.57); the real states with the top 16 per position Gaussianised fall below the Gaussian cloud (criterion 0.72, retention 0.16); the tail's mass reads entry (breadth 0.86 within the band S 0.9-1.1), not its shape (kurtosis 0.58) (e544).
- The set is row-wise: the grafted pairs moved to random positions at every checkpoint give the same vocabulary (retention 0.51, 94 entries, gate 0.89) with the per-position persistence gone (0.58 at 1.1-1.2, the maximum at the same position in 0.02 of cases); scrambling the checkpoints keeps the set's long-range overlap (retention 0.47) and loses its consecutive coherence (125 entries, gate 0.71). The vocabulary is the slow evolution of the set of rows the states make extreme somewhere (e544b).
- Each word's extremes are written by its own persistent ensemble: an extreme is made by 9232 effective MLP writers in a cancelling mass (the ten largest 0.01 of the absolute contribution, the thousand largest positive contributors 8.4 times the net) with 0.57 of the projection transported by attention; the same population writes a row's extremes wherever they occur (writer-vector cosine 0.489 within a row at step 8000, 0.597 at 16000, against 0.001 across rows and 0.024 against a typical position), random directions have none (0.083), the ensembles are not shared (effective rank 63.6 of 247 words) and they persist (0.882 from step 8000 to 16000 against 0.002 for different words) (e545).

## How the results flow

- `e541b → e542 → e543`. The cloud's persistence taken apart: broad, its dynamic modes its variance modes, the native extremes persisting beyond the bulk; and a Gaussian cloud with the real covariance and any temporal dynamics gives the spatial and temporal persistence but not the plateau or the retention.
- `e543 → e544 → e544b`. Where the native fifth is: the top four projections per position, grafted, carry it whole and the bulk carries nothing; and it is the set of extreme rows that matters, not their positions.
- `e544b → e545`. Who writes the extremes: thousands of writers in a cancelling mass, the same population at all of a row's extremes and across training, specific to the row, not shared across words.
