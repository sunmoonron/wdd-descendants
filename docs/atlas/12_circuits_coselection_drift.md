# 12. Phase 2: known circuits, co-selection, drift and curvature (S32)

**Question.** Do the program's unit statistics and the co-selection signal proposed in the post "What if not Circuits?" see known circuits, and how do neurons and structures change across training?
**Established.** The program's unit statistics detect the induction and IOI circuits in all five models but barely see them in natural-text averages, so the old natural-text negatives (e264, e302) describe averages (e357, e358, e366). The post's co-selection signal is mostly layer structure (e359), finds induction heads in two to three of five models (e363, e368), and peaks while a circuit is being built, because at an optimum mean first-order selection vanishes while use does not (e370b). Ablation interactions are 2.4-48 times the local curvature because the loss along a head's scale has a knee (e371, e372), which session 33 traced mostly to the softmax readout, the same readout that inflated phase 2's redundancy (e374, e380, e384). Writes settle before corrected logit signatures in Pythia and OLMo, though late halves still co-adapt (e360, e360d, e362d, e378), and co-selection structure and the quotient's coordinate are re-formed rather than carried (e364b, e365b).
**Start here:** e357, e363, e370b, e371, e360 · **Sessions:** S1 (e81), S2-3 (e142), S32 · **Scripts:** `scripts/e81_ckpt_drift.py`, `scripts/e142_phenotype_training.py`, `scripts/e357_*.py` to `e373_*.py`

## How the results flow

- `e357 → e358 → e366 → e384`: the unit statistics pass both positive controls on task data, but the same heads are nearly invisible in natural-text averages, which reopens e264 and e302 as blind-spot results; scoring at induction-applicable positions recovers part of the signal; the S34 audit re-read e357 and e366 on the centred logit (redundancy shrinks, the contrast survives).
- `e359 → e363 → e368 → e369 → e370 → e370b → e379`: batch co-selection is layer structure and its induction test was circular, so e363 defined the circuit by attention; token resolution helps in three models; ablation co-effects find GPT-2's circuit where the gradient misses it; saturation does not explain the miss; mean selection vanishes at an optimum, as toys later reproduce.
- `e363 → e368 → e369` over Pythia and OLMo checkpoints, and `e359 → e364 → e364b`: co-selection of the induction circuit rises as it forms and fades as training converges (not monotonically at OLMo's token level), and the co-selection matrix as a whole is re-formed rather than carried.
- `e371 → e372 → e374`: gradient and curvature at the trained point underpredict ablation interactions; the loss along a head's scale is flat then steep; session 33 found the loss quadratic at full strength and the knee mostly the softmax readout.
- `e81 → e360 → e360b → e360d → e360e → e378`: late write directions are stable (phase 1); per-neuron drift over 46 checkpoints; raw signature statistics sat below the reliability ceiling and were replaced by corrected curves; precision was ruled out; stitching then killed the reading that settled lower layers are swappable.
- `e360 → e362 → e362b → e362d`, with `e360c`: OLMo repeats writes-before-signatures over a longer run; importance turns over late in both models; clusters split and merge equally.
- `e365 → e365b → e373 → e380`: the quotient is born at the induction transition, does not persist in function space, and leans on the induction heads only just after the transition; e380 re-read e365's redundancy growth as loss convexity.
- `e286, e317, e340, e346 → e361`: the session-31 quotient battery re-run at four birth depths removes the block-2 caveat.
- `e130 → e142`: the identifiability phenotype is largely set by step 16000 (Spearman 0.83 with the final).
