# 65. The brake and the bypass (S157)

**Question.** Session 156 found the crowd's net sum opposing a neuron's largest inputs off its class, and the weak activations' write living in sixteen directions per block. Is the brake what a pruned graph loses, is it learned, can the envelope be predicted from the block input without running the weak neurons, and does the ledger hold at 7B and 14B once the model runs in fp32?

**Established.**
- The brake is causal: swapped in only off the class the sixteen-input simulation carries 0.52 to 0.88 of its damage, sixteen excitatory wires alone cost 5.88 to 561.34 of the ablation damage over all positions, and neither sixteen inhibitory wires (0.59 to 1.06) nor the upstream weak-write bus (0.29 to 0.93) restores it: reading: the brake is causal and it is position-specific: swapping the sixteen-input simulation in only off a neuron's top-5% positions carries 0.78 to 0.88 of its all-position damage at the mid-depth blocks (0.52 to 0.64 at the sixth blocks), while at those positions alone it carries 0.10 to 0.20; sixteen excitatory wires by themselves fire the neuron everywhere, costing 6 to 561 times the ablation damage over all positions; adding the sixteen strongest inhibitory wires, which carry 0.87 to 1.35 of the net opposition at each position, halves the all-position damage at mid-depth (3.0 from 5.6 and 7.0 in Pythia-410m and Qwen2.5-0.5B at block 12) but leaves the firings at 0.59 to 1.06 of the ablation damage, because a few wires cannot cancel a large excitation to the small net the model has; the crowd's write on the upstream blocks' weak-write subspaces (sixteen directions per block, e693's bus, 96 to 288 directions) halves both the firing and the all-position ratios at the four mid-depth blocks where the stream's own directions did nothing in e692 (0.35 to 0.93 at the firings from 0.74 to 1.95, 1.1 to 2.6 over all positions from 4.2 to 7.0), and signed wires with the bus is the best training-free graph everywhere (0.37 to 0.73 at the firings, 0.62 to 1.75 over all positions) without reaching the essay's almost no error anywhere; so the error node a pruned neuron graph needs is the brake, the brake lives in the weak-write subspace of the upstream blocks, and sixteen wires of either sign cannot carry it (e697).
- The brake is learned: at step 1000 the crowd's net sum off a neuron's class is 0.28 to 0.30 of its sixteen wires' and in the final model -0.76 to -0.60, falling monotonically (Spearman -1.00 to -0.99 in 3 seeds), while the wires' net share at the core rises from 0.48 to 0.56 to 0.75 to 0.86: reading: the brake is learned, early and gradually, and the drive sparsifies with it: at step 1000 of Pythia-160m the crowd's net sum off a neuron's class reinforces its sixteen wires (+0.28 to +0.30 of theirs), the sign flips by step 2000 (-0.15 to -0.19), the brake reaches -0.43 to -0.48 by step 8000, -0.50 to -0.53 by 16000 and -0.60 to -0.76 at the end of training, monotonically in three seeds (Spearman -0.99 to -1.00), while at the core the crowd goes from reinforcing (+0.69 to +0.93) to near neutral (+0.14 to +0.22) and the sixteen wires' net share of the drive rises from 0.48 to 0.56 to 0.75 to 0.86; the 1 to 5 percent shell's adjacency to the core dips from 0.57 at step 1000 to 0.46 at 2000 and recovers to 0.55 to 0.58; so training builds the sparse drive and the collective brake together, the same process e676 saw as growth from the centre, and the final model's crowd is the residue of that process rather than noise (e701).
- The weak envelope is largely linear in the block input: a rank-16 bypass predicted from the normalised input, with the weak neurons not run, recovers 0.56 to 0.96 of the zeroing cost across 8 models against 0.66 to 0.96 for the oracle sixteen, and ARC-Easy moves by -0.09 to -0.01 under it against -0.30 to -0.04 under zeroing: reading: the weak envelope is mostly a linear function of the block's normalised input: a rank-16 bypass solved in closed form on held-out text, added at every block at once with the weak neurons not run, recovers 0.56 to 0.96 of the zeroing cost in eight models against 0.66 to 0.96 for the oracle projection of the true weak write, halves the cost in every model, and the full linear prediction matches the oracle sixteen in seven of eight; the linear map explains only 0.18 to 0.34 of the weak write's variance, so the predictable part is the part that matters; on ARC-Easy zeroing costs 4 to 30 points of accuracy and the bypass 1 to 9 (within 2 in GPT-2 medium, GPT-2 large and Qwen2.5-1.5B), so the essay's ablatable weak activations are replaceable by sixteen numbers per block computed from the input, which is a locally linear reading of the weak regime and not a sparse one (e696).
- In fp32 the ledger is exact at scale: on Qwen2.5-7B every input reproduces the neuron (0.00) and the sixteen largest inputs, carrying 0.02 of it, cost 1.27 of the ablation damage at the strong firings of the neurons that matter most: reading: with the model in fp32 the ledger is exact at 7B and says what it said at 400M: on Qwen2.5-7B at block 14 every input reproduces the neuron (0.00 of the ablation damage, split error 5e-6, noise floor 0.000) and for the twenty neurons with the largest ablation KL among a hundred the sixteen largest inputs carry 0.017 of the input and cost 1.27 of the ablation damage at the strong firings (2.6 over all positions), 256 inputs 1.32; the bf16 runs failed the same check at 0.86 to 0.95 because the residual stream is rounded to bf16 block by block, so the exact split cannot be scored against a bf16 model; at 14B the fp32 weights alone take 59 GB and the ledger did not fit beside them on an 80 GB GPU with sixteen or with eight windows (e700).

## How the results flow

- `64 e695 → e697, e701`: the crowd as suppressor, read from the net ledger, now tested by swapping the pruned simulation in only on or only off the class, by signed wires and a weak-write bus, and followed over Pythia's checkpoints.
- `64 e693, e693b → e696`: the sixteen-direction weak envelope, now predicted from the block input by a closed-form linear map and substituted for the weak neurons at every block.
- `64 e694, 62 e685 → e700`: the ledger's simulation at 7B and 14B, with the bf16 rounding of the stream removed by running the model in fp32.
