# 67. The gated remainder and the strong field (S159)

**Question.** Area 66 left the field as a second atom with three open edges: the gated models' remainder after the linear bypass, the field's dependence on the corpus, and the strong write, whose directions the readers were never offered. Is the remainder a low-order function of the block's input, does the field survive a change of corpus, does the kept quarter of each block's activations have a field of its own, and when does OLMo's brake cross a half?

**Established.**
- The strong field: beside the weak field it brings sixteen wires from 0.41 to 1.95 to 0.12 to 0.53 of the ablation damage at the firings (weak alone 0.29 to 0.93, strong alone 0.28 to 0.94, a random subspace of the same dimension 0.27 to 1.15) and from 0.83 to 7.00 to 0.26 to 1.45 over all positions: reading: the strong write has a field of its own, and the two fields together carry most of what sixteen wires miss: with sixteen directions per upstream block for the kept quarter of its activations beside the weak field's sixteen, the ablation ratio at a neuron's firings goes from 0.41 to 1.95 with the wires alone to 0.12 to 0.53 with both fields, where the weak field alone leaves 0.29 to 0.93 and the strong field alone 0.28 to 0.94; the two fields overlap by 0.28 to 0.54, so they are different subspaces of the stream, each worth about as much as the other to the readers and worth more together. Both fields beat a random subspace of their dimension by 0.08 to 0.62, by under 0.2 at four blocks of eight, two of them (Pythia-410m block 18 and SmolLM2-135M block 15) where that dimension, thirty-two per upstream block, is over half the stream's; the attention units as exact wires still add nothing over both fields. Over all positions both fields bring the sixteen-wire ratio from 0.83 to 7.00 to 0.26 to 1.45, halving it at five blocks of eight. What remains of a pruned neuron's error, an eighth to a half, is the part of each upstream write outside its block's thirty-two directions. (e706).
- The gated remainder: a closed-form quadratic bypass recovers 0.57 to 0.67 of the zeroing cost in the gated models against 0.56 to 0.70 for the linear one, a Pile-fitted linear bypass transfers at 0.38 to 0.59 out of domain against 0.35 to 0.60 for the wikitext fit, and the field's directions on wikitext and the Pile overlap by 0.58 to 0.71: reading: the gated remainder is not a quadratic of the block's input in random directions: 256 random bilinear features solved in closed form beside the linear ones change the recovery by at most 0.03 in every model, on wikitext and out of domain, and in OLMo-1B make it worse (0.67 against 0.70), so what the linear bypass misses in the gated models (0.07 to 0.22 of the zeroing cost below the oracle sixteen) is either of higher order, specific to directions random features do not find, or not a function of the block's input at all. Fitted on a mixed Pile stream the bypass is no better and no worse than fitted on wikitext, in domain or out, so the fit does not depend on the corpus. The field itself is only partly stable across corpora: its sixteen directions on wikitext and on the Pile overlap by 0.58 to 0.71 (chance 16/D, under 0.02), so the field has a core the corpora share and a remainder they do not, and the bypass transfers through the core: out of domain it recovers 0.35 to 0.93 of the zeroing cost, 0.59 to 1.00 of its in-domain recovery, under 0.7 only in Qwen2.5-1.5B. (e705).
- OLMo-1B's brake crosses -0.5 at step 512000 and 1000000 at blocks 12 and 8 (-0.34 to -0.27 of the wires at step 32000, Spearman -1.00 to -1.00 with the step) (e701, OLM: reading: in OLMo-1B the brake forms over the whole run rather than at its start: at blocks 8 and 12 the crowd's net sum over the sixteen wires in the bottom half of positions is -0.27 and -0.34 of theirs at step 32000, -0.33 and -0.44 at step 128000, -0.41 and -0.52 at step 512000, -0.51 and -0.61 at step 1000000 and -0.56 and -0.69 in the final model, monotone in the step at both blocks (Spearman -1.00) and crossing -0.5 at step 512000 at block 12 (about a third of the run by tokens) and at step 1000000 at block 8 (about two thirds), where Pythia-160m crossed at step 16000 of 143000; the wires' net share at the core rises to 0.76 and 0.71 by step 256000 and 64000 and falls back to 0.62 and 0.52 as the brake deepens, so the later training moves part of the core's drive out of the sixteen wires and into the crowd while it deepens the brake off the class. (e701l).
