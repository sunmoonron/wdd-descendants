# 63. The already-clean debate II: growth, facts, knots, explanations and memorised text (S155)

**Question.** The essay's developmental claims: neurons grow by recursive generalization from a local origin, consolidation is for readers (buildability), facts ride related machinery, memorisation is late and semantic, arbitrary packing is unstable, and an explanation that fits the strong activations is enough. Tested over Pythia's checkpoints, with learned facts, a planted knot and held-out text.

**Established.**
- The sixteen neurons that write a learned fact's answer fire on natural text about the fact's value or relation 0.42 to 0.56 of the time (random neurons 0.14 to 0.16), facts sharing a value share writers (Jaccard 0.34 to 0.38 against 0.00 to 0.04 across relations), random codes' writers are related to code-like text 0.25 to 0.59 of the time, and no row is rewritten: reading: a learned fact's writers are neither an arbitrary store nor the fact's own circuit: facts that share a value share a third of their sixteen writers (Jaccard 0.34 in Pythia-410m, 0.38 in Qwen2.5-0.5B) and facts of different relations almost none (0.00, 0.04), the structure of a map of values and relations rather than a key-value database; 42 and 56 percent of a fact's writers fire on natural text about its value or relation against 14 and 16 percent for random neurons, three to four times chance and a majority only in Qwen; the random codes ride a shared code route (their writers overlap among themselves, 0.13 and 0.23, more than facts of one relation do) and fire on code-like text twice to three times as often as chance; everything sits in the last quarter of the network, no write column moves by more than a thousandth in cosine, and nearly half the writers were already the base model's strongest at the same query, Cammarata's off-ramp on existing routes rather than a new neuron, with routes that are related to the fact more often than chance and, in Pythia, unrelated more often than not (e682).
- A neuron's new contexts arrive next to a recent offshoot no more often than the members' ages predict (chain enrichment 0.91 to 0.92 over three seeds) and almost never as jumps (0.01 to 0.02 below the random-position null): growth is adjacent but not chain-like, and the root is more token-concentrated than the final class: reading: growth is adjacent but not a chain: a new context's nearest existing member by input attribution is a recent entrant exactly as often as the members' ages predict (enrichment 0.91 to 0.92 in three seeds, above 1.5 for no neuron) and is below the random-position null in 1 to 2 percent of entries, in attribution space and in state space alike; the essay's branch of branches ('iy' to 'diy' to 'do it yourself') does not appear as an order of arrivals in Pythia-160m's top-1% classes, while its 'nothing unrelated' does; the root at step 1000 is at most a little more token-concentrated than the final class, and the minor route of a two-route neuron entered by chain no more than the core; one caveat bounds the test: half of a top-1% class turns over each checkpoint, so most entries are margin churn, and a magnitude-defined core would be the sharper test (e683).
- Across recruitment a word's row gains downstream readers relative to norm-matched control rows on the same positions in 0.75 to 0.79 of words (ten strongest readers' mean share) and 0.75 to 0.81 (reader mass), the readers aligning with the row more than with the class direction in 0.91 to 0.97, while sub-class specialists do not multiply (0.41 to 0.49 above the null): reading: readers follow recruitment, weakly: the ten strongest downstream readers of a recruited row gain share across the recruitment relative to norm-matched rows on the same positions in three quarters to four fifths of words, the total reader mass likewise, and the readers' alignment moves toward the row rather than the class direction in nine tenths or more; but no reader ever depends on the row at a fifth of its own spread (the counts stay at zero before and after), and sub-class specialists do not multiply beyond chance; so the essay's buildability is visible as a slow redistribution of many readers' weights toward the recruited parameter, not as the arrival of a dedicated reader, which is what e591's 'recruitment is not a performance event' looks like from the readers' side (e684).
- A surface explanation of a neuron's class (token, previous token, bigram) covers 0.00 to 0.01 of its held-out positions at precision 0.5 and reaches recall 0.9 only at precision 0.01 to 0.01, 0.09 to 0.14 of positions having no training precedent: reading: a neuron's class is contextual, not lexical: tables of the current token, the previous token and the bigram, fitted to the class itself with about 350 entries, cover 0 to 4 percent of its held-out positions at precision 0.5, reaching recall 0.9 costs all precision, a tenth of held-out positions carry no training precedent at all, and the most common token covers a tenth of a class; so an explanation that fits a neuron's examples can only be a pattern over context, Gao's recall question cannot be answered from surface features, and the essay's agents are right to write sentences rather than token lists, which this test does not score (e687).
- A hand-made knot (gender and number pinned onto one row of Pythia-160m before either handle exists) installs in 4 of 5 seeds and after 6000 steps of ordinary training keeps both votes in 4 and sheds one in 0, the dials ending at -0.00 to 0.06 (gender) and -0.00 to 0.06 (number); single-concept pins keep theirs in 4 of 4: reading: as a description, arbitrary packing is stable: a quiet row pinned to carry both gender and number at step 8000 takes both votes in four of five seeds and keeps both through 6000 steps of the next-token loss alone in every seed, exactly as the single-concept pins keep theirs, so the essay's semantic gravity does not unpack a knot that gradient descent did not make; as a mechanism, the pin mostly installs a description: where it created a dial (three of five knots, two of four singles) the dial decays through the release by the same factor in knots and singles, and in the rest no dial ever existed, e627's lesson again; so the knot test refutes instability and cannot separate packed from single on behaviour (e688).
- Memorised sequences are recited by 153 to 310 distinct writers at depth 0.83 to 0.93 (controls 172 to 384 at 0.78 to 1.00), whose classes are no less coherent and which fire on the content's field 0.34 to 0.39 of the time: reading: memorised text is recited by the ordinary machinery: in Pythia-410m and Pythia-1B its writers sit at the same depth as a low-loss window's (0.83 against 0.83, 0.93 against 0.93), are as many (310 against 304, 310 against 315), have classes as coherent (6.9 against 6.8 bits, 6.7 against 6.3) and fire on the content's field as often (0.39 against 0.41, 0.38 against 0.40), and in Pythia-160m they are slightly shallower and fewer than both controls; nothing in the ledger marks the recited token's writers as special, which is what riding existing routes looks like from the writes, so the essay's 'local and semantic' and Nanda's 'needs superposition' are both unresolved by writer statistics and need a mechanism test (e690).
