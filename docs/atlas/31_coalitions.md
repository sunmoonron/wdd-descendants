# 31. The coalitions: who writes a word's extremes, and that the coalition precedes the word (S94-S95)

**Question.** Sessions 92-93 located the native fifth in the top four projections at each position and found the vocabulary to be the set of rows the states make extreme somewhere. What makes a row extreme at a position: a few large writes, a stable population of writers, or a few shared write programs; is the population specific to the row and persistent through training; and does it exist before the row becomes a word, or only describe the extreme afterward?

**Established.**
- Each word's extremes are written by its own persistent ensemble: an extreme is made by 9232 effective MLP writers in a cancelling mass (the ten largest 0.01 of the absolute contribution, the thousand largest positive contributors 8.4 times the net) with 0.57 of the projection transported by attention; the same population writes a row's extremes wherever they occur (writer-vector cosine 0.489 within a row at step 8000, 0.597 at 16000, against 0.001 across rows and 0.024 against a typical position), random directions have none (0.083), the ensembles are not shared (effective rank 63.6 of 247 words) and they persist (0.882 from step 8000 to 16000 against 0.002 for different words) (e545).
- The coalition precedes the word: four intervals before entry, at the positions that will be its extremes, a future entrant's writer vectors already cohere across positions at 0.42 (at entry 0.50), where a near miss matched on S one interval before the event coheres at 0.20 throughout, the contexts alone at 0.25 against 0.16 and their stability generic (0.72 against 0.69); both have S 1.12 at k=-1, the entrant crosses to 1.27, the near miss falls back to 1.02. What rises across the entry is the coherence of the contributions (2.45 to 3.72, the near miss's 2.47 at its matched moment collapsing to 0.64), the own neuron's contribution (0.06 to 0.12) and the alignment. The ensembles are private (a word's aggregate ensemble write has cosine 0.575 with its own row against 0.013 with other words, the own row the largest for 0.95) and persist beyond their contexts (real 0.873 from step 8000 to 16000 against a selection-matched null of 0.599 and the activations alone 0.787) (e546).
- The rise of an entrant's projection from k=-4 to entry, additively: a quarter the row's rotation (0.25), a quarter the attention outputs' own motion (0.25, transported writes arriving), 0.13 the local writers' motion, the activations' change -0.06; the approach and the crossing have the same shape (e546b).

## How the results flow

- `e544b → e545`. Who writes the extremes: thousands of writers in a cancelling mass, the same population at all of a row's extremes and across training, specific to the row, not shared across words.
- `e545 → e546 → e546b`. Before or after: the coalition is in place four intervals before entry where a near miss matched on S has none; what rises is coherence and alignment, a quarter of the rise the row's rotation and a quarter the arrival of transported writes.
