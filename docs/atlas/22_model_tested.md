# 22. The extreme-value model tested: the floor, the chord and the crowd (S64-S68)

**Question.** THEORY 3m said an atom is a word when its projection clears the Gumbel level of the competing atoms, a writer through its own coefficient and a non-writer through the second-order alignment of the state cloud with the atoms. Do those two numbers, computed with nothing fitted, reproduce the observed maxima state by state, and if not, what carries the maximum?

**Established.**
- The floor is right: the rotated dictionary's maximum is 0.97-1.00 of the exact Gaussian extreme-value mean computed from the atoms' second moment along the state, in every model, block and checkpoint (27 cells) (e503, e506).
- The numerator is not: the non-writers' maximum stands 1.3-2.3 times above the level from their own second moment while the second-order alignment is a factor 1.02-1.24 (the accent is a tail, arriving between Pythia steps 256 and 512), and the writers' maximum is 1.5-2.2 times the largest write's own coefficient; prominence against the floor predicts writer wins at 0.45 against 0.78 (e503).
- A word's projection is its own coefficient (0.3-0.7) plus the position's other large writes through their cosines (0.3-0.7), the chord, less the crowd of small MLP writes (-0.05 to -0.5), plus attention and the centring; the top non-writer atom is the chord's shadow in GPT-2 and OLMo (0.56-0.93 cross terms, cosine 0.3-0.6 with a writer) and attention's in Pythia; at Pythia step 512 the maxima are head atoms carried by the dense sum of small writes, and by the end the sparse chord carries 0.8-0.9 (e505).
- Summed as written, the largest writes overshoot the maximum twofold where they are as large as the state (GPT-2, OLMo) and name the atom in 2-16% of states; the overshoot is the common direction they share, and centred the chord predicts GPT-2's maximum within 1-13%, ranks states at 0.56-0.75 and names the atom in 32-46% (top four 54-64%); in OLMo the crowd's contraction is needed (all writes 0.78-0.86 of the level) and in Pythia the small writes add (all writes within 1-28%); over training the chord reproduces the words clock and not the accent (e506).

- Against the whole profile the centred chord is a partial predictor: in GPT-2 calibrated within 13-25% on its own top 200 (R^2 0.54-0.66), the largest atom recovered in 32-46% of states, a third of the top ten, the true top hundred ordered at 0.30-0.41; the crowd takes 0.16-0.31 of it and attention gives back 0.13-0.16. The crowd flips from amplifier to contractor over Pythia's training (gain along the chord +1.37 at step 512 to -0.64 at the end at block 12) while attention's gain rises from 0.08 to 0.30-0.50; at step 512 the state is its MLP write-sum (R^2 0.98-0.99 for the whole profile) (e507).

- The chord's clean maximum in GPT-2 is extreme-value selection over a nearly exchangeable residual: a null that keeps the chord and permutes the residual reproduces the maximum's level within 2%, the winner's rank within 100 and the winner's residual excess; the chord's top atom is calibrated. In OLMo the residual contracts the chord's extremes specifically, in Pythia's middle training it reinforces them (e508).
- The crowd's contraction of the chord is the network's response to the chord (removing the chord from what the MLPs see removes 1.2-1.9 times the actual gain), born and growing with area 05's learned contraction (Pythia steps 512 to 4000 to the end); a random direction of the chord's size is contracted two to three times more than the chord, so the contraction spares the vocabulary; the early amplification is not a response; the chord's own rows come to be excited by it (e509).

- The contraction does not know the atoms: as a linear response it is isotropic within 25% across native rows, embeddings, head bases, random, covariance-matched and principal directions, native rows slightly more contracted than random (1.01-1.17), used rows like unused, born isotropic between Pythia steps 512 and 4000; the chord's sparing is a nonlinear, chord-amplitude, pattern-level effect (used rows at the state's size contracted 0.63-0.80 of random in GPT-2, 0.66 at Pythia's block 18, 1.02-1.19 in OLMo, the model-by-model pattern of e508); at step 512 the MLP amplifies on-manifold directions only (e510).

- The sparing is write history, not the vocabulary's manifold: fake chords of native rows with the position's coefficients and norm, random or the most-used, are contracted like a random direction (GPT-2, Pythia step 16000) or most of the way to it (OLMo, Pythia's end); only the rows that wrote at the position are spared (e511).

## How the results flow

- `e483, e493, e498 → e503`. The calibrated floor (e483) and the two-term decomposition (e493, e498) gave the model two numbers; e503 computes them per state and finds the floor right and both terms wrong by 1.5-2.
- `e510 → e511`. Fake chords of native rows, even the most-used, are contracted like random directions; only the position's actual writes are spared.
- `e509 → e510`. The sparing of the chord is not a property of native directions: the linear contraction is isotropic and blind to the atoms; the chord is spared as a large in-distribution pattern, in GPT-2 and late Pythia, and targeted in OLMo.
- `e507 → e508, e509`. The selection null explains GPT-2's clean maximum and exposes OLMo's targeted contraction and Pythia's mid-training reinforcement; the ablation shows the contraction is the MLPs' response to the chord, born with area 05's contraction and weaker on the chord than on any direction.
- `e506 → e507`. The chord against the whole profile: partial in GPT-2, worse than the write-sum there and better than it in OLMo; through Pythia's training the crowd's gain along the chord flips sign, which is where the learned contraction of area 05 may enter.
- `e503 → e505 → e506`. The exact split of the winning projection shows the chord and the crowd; the prediction from the ledger alone then fails for the chord as written, succeeds for the centred chord in GPT-2, and needs the crowd in OLMo and the small writes in Pythia.
