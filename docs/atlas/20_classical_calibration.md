# 20. Calibration against classical theory: extreme values, erasure, bits, growth, streams, pursuit, components, timescales, the factor's origin, the two clocks (S58-S62)

**Question.** Read as an instrument rather than a claim, what is WDD? Session 58 calibrated it against theory older than OMP, on the five original models: the extreme-value law of its detection threshold (Fisher and Tippett 1928, Gumbel 1935), the erasure channel for its code (Shannon 1948), bits per word (Fano 1961), Heaps' law for its vocabulary (Herdan 1960), the sequential entropy of its word stream (Shannon 1951), projection pursuit for its atoms as directions (Friedman and Tukey 1974), independent components for their rediscovery (Comon 1994), mutual coherence for its supports (Tropp 2004), and Prony's method (1795) for the timescales of provenance loss.

**Established.**
- The detection threshold is an extreme-value law. The largest competitor correlation is a Gumbel variable in all five models, its mean grows as sqrt(2 ln m), and its two fitted parameters predict the detection curve of an injected write within 0.1; the resolution is a write of 0.25-0.4 of the state's norm (e483).
- The scale of that law is provenance. For a rotated dictionary it equals the atoms' second moment exactly (ratio 0.96-1.02); for the native dictionary it is 2.9-6.2 times that. This provenance factor is 6-14 at block 1, falls with depth to 3.5-5 in four models, and rises through Pythia's training from 2.2-3.0 at step 1000 (e483, e492).
- The description is a non-redundant code with correction. Every word matters, a random half refitted reaches 0.59-0.80 of OMP's own eight, yet refitting native words after erasure recovers 0.02-0.09 of loss recovered where refitting rotated words costs (e484). The supports are equally near-orthogonal for both dictionaries, so the correction lies in what the words fit, not in their geometry (e490).
- The first native word carries 0.4-1.2 bits about the next token (rotated 0.2-0.3; the current token 0.9-1.4) and 1.6-4.4 bits about the current token: lexical first (e485). The word stream has 0.2-0.9 bits of sequential structure per position, half of the text's; the rotated stream has almost no entropy (e487). The Heaps exponent is the same with and without provenance; provenance concentrates usage on 2-5% of the words used (e486).
- The rows are the heavy-tailed directions of the state cloud (kurtosis 2-10 times rotated at the 90th percentile; OMP's usage follows it, e488), and FastICA on the states alone lands on them at 2-3 times the rotated rows' match and 1.7-2 times the principal directions', in part: its components are built from several rows (e489).
- The provenance factor decomposes into write sparsity and an accent: at early and middle depth the competitor maximum is one of the position's largest writes in 73-100% of positions and the writers alone reproduce the factor; non-writing rows sit 1.3-1.9 above the rotated level. Through Pythia's training the writers' part rises from 1.0-1.5 to 1.6-2.6 while the non-writers' part is there at step 1000 and hardly grows: the vocabulary forms as the largest writes become prominent (e493).
- The vocabulary's two components have different clocks in Pythia: the accent (non-writing rows collectively aligned with the states) forms by step 512; the words (the largest writes standing out) cross the rotated level near step 1000 and grow to the end (e492, e493). They are two different quantities: across 45 checkpoint-block cells the writers' part tracks write sparsity (the top write's prominence +0.86, the effective number of writes -0.81) and nothing else, while the non-writers' part tracks the alignment of the states' covariance with the atoms' (+0.61) and the concentration of the state cloud, and neither predicts the other at the next checkpoint (e498). OLMo shows the same two clocks, with the accent decaying after its early peak. The words are MLP rows at every checkpoint, though attention comes to carry most of the state's energy (e500).
- Provenance loss has one timescale: a single exponential with a per-block retention of 0.71-0.88 fits the recall of a block's top write over the next eight blocks, and Prony's second mode carries no amplitude (e491).

## How the results flow

- `e56, e127 → e483 → e492 → e493`. The prominence law said a write is read when it beats a competitor level; e483 gives that level its distribution (Gumbel), its growth (sqrt(2 ln m)) and a calibration curve, and separates its scale into a geometric part, exact for a rotated dictionary, and a provenance factor. e492 follows the factor across depth and training: largest at the first block, falling with depth, rising as the vocabulary forms (e443's accent is already a factor of 2-3 at step 1000). e493 splits the factor: the writers' part is the largest writes standing out, which forms through training; the non-writers' part is the accent, present from step 512. e498 shows the two parts move with different quantities, write sparsity and second-order alignment, and neither leads the other.
- The SAE bridge that started in this area (e494) has its own page, [21 SAE bridge](21_sae_bridge.md).
- `e465, e466 → e484 → e490`. What a description omits is functional (e465, e466); e484 shows the code has no redundancy but has correction, and e490 shows the correction is not in the supports' geometry.
- `e03, e196 → e488 → e489`. The own-word edge lives in the first few atoms (e03), and selection is predicted by covariance-matched noise only partly (e196). e488 names the rows' distinguishing property in the state cloud, heavy tails, and e489 shows a method that looks for heavy tails without the weights finds them in part.
- `e151, e169, e439, e459 → e485, e487, e486`. Support overlap predicted the next token (e151); the words are partly the lexicon (e439); words persist across positions (e459). Measured in bits, the first word is lexical first and predictive second, the stream has half the text's sequential structure, and provenance concentrates usage without changing the growth law.
- `e21, e131, e188, e194 → e491`. The half-life of about two blocks and the per-block contraction gain (e194: retention 0.58-0.84) are one process: a single exponential fits, and its retention (0.71-0.88) is the contraction's.
