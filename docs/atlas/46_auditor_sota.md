# 46. The auditor against the state of the art (S115)

**Question.** Is the writers' activation a usable unlearning auditor, or a correlation? Three tests: whether a threshold tuned on four methods flags a fifth; how it fares against the auditors the field uses, on the field's benchmark; and whether it predicts an attack that needs no training, a page of the forgotten domain in the prompt.

**Established.**
- A rule for one question (e616): tuned on four methods the writers' activation flags the fifth as shallow (a 20-step relearning recovering 0.8 of the rise) at 0.89 accuracy, AUC 0.93, majority 0.57; the difference-selected set 0.78, ratio-selected 0.83, the probe 0.84, the still-writing share 0.33. For how many steps a relearning takes the forget rise is the rule (0.81) and the writers reach 0.71.
- On TOFU (e613, e613b) on TOFU the forget set is not a domain in the dictionary's sense (2 of 256 words pass the forget threshold; the 32 most forget-leaning words average 0.62 forget share where a neutral word sits at 0.52) and the audit has nothing to read: the ranked writers fire at 0.99 to 1.00 after every method, so their 0.76 correlation with the recovery is the ordering of RMU's 1.00 against 0.99 and carries nothing; the auditors with signal there are the output KL (0.82) and the representation drift (0.76), which rank RMU (recovery 1.00 in 10 steps) above NPO (0.70) and ascent (0.57), while the membership AUC (-0.57) and the probe (-0.86) point the wrong way. On tofu two forget pairs in the prompt recover 0.26 / 0.22 / 0.39 of the rise after ascent / npo / rmu.
- In context (e614): on the Pile no prompt undoes a weight change at 160m, 410m and 1B: a page of the forgotten domain (PubMed / Github) recovers at 160m -0.03 / -0.67 after ascent, 0.02 / 0.01 after NPO, -0.02 / -0.12 after RMU; at 410m -0.21 / -0.67 after ascent, -0.01 / -0.66 after NPO, -0.04 / -0.22 after RMU; at 1B -0.06 / -1.39 after ascent, 0.08 / -0.98 after NPO, -0.13 / -0.37 after RMU, deepening ascent's rise instead (up to +0.94 nats beyond the original's rise under the same page, 160m github), while an off-domain page recovers 0.03 to 0.35; the silenced writers stay quiet in context (0.69 to 0.64 after ascent on PubMed at 160m). The suppression is keyed to the context it was trained in, and the task, not the size, is what a prompt can reach.
