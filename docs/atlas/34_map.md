# 34. The map: nine directions the record had not covered, run in one session (S101)

**Question.** A twenty-one-direction map of where the program could go (measurement theory, the parameter-activation bridge, other modalities and architectures, training dynamics and optimizer geometry, cross-model correspondence, merging and transplant, fine-tuning, robustness and hallucination, automated hypothesis generation, scaling the instrument, information theory, identifiability, model edits, invariances, ontology) was triaged against the 533 experiments. Eight were already covered (areas 06, 07, 16, 17 and 23: the light cones, the word-for-word translation test e445 and the concept-level correspondence e453, the forensic uses e456-e462, the grokking toy e444), four are analytic, and nine were open. All nine were run here, each with the follow-up a critic would ask for.

**Established.**
- The vocabulary is a property of writing through rows to a residual stream: a state-space model with neither attention nor MLPs has it more strongly than Pythia-160m (advantage 0.258 against 0.188, words at S 1.78 against 0.99 rotated; Mamba-370m alike), two vision transformers have it without tokens (advantage 0.105 / 0.161, broader words, a looser floor), and the grokking toy has it under AdamW, sign momentum and clipped SGD alike (0.76 / 0.73 / 0.72; a memorising run 0.12).
- It is conserved across scale by its context sets (0.40 of 410m's words twinned in 160m, null 0.03; the same across a data twin; three quarters of the within-run rate), mostly not as token identities (twinned purity 0.30); within a run the context sets outlive the rows (0.55 twins between step 16000 and the end against 0.21 by row index); the twins are the older words.
- Entry is the row's turn into a direction the cloud already speaks (the 16000 direction at 1.29 in the 8000 cloud; row part +0.15, cloud part +0.03), exit the cloud's erosion; a transplanted whole neuron brings its word (0.68-0.80 of entrants); a merge along the trajectory has no barrier and half its words from both parents.
- Two nulls: no single state, and so no ledger, announces an upstream corruption beyond two tokens; the ledger does not see a factual error where the confidence does (AUC 0.85); invented names are more spoken, not less.
- The instrument scales: 1024 candidates by projection reproduce OMP exactly at 1.9-4.6 times the speed; the early blocks' rows are needed.
- Continued training moves the states, not the rows (0.0001 rotation for 2.1 nats); the word set is a marginal statistic (0.86 under noise costing +0.03 nats), the usage correlation the robust one.

## How the results flow

- `e562, e563, e556`: the object widened. Vision transformers (no tokens), state-space models (no attention, no MLP) and three optimizers all give the native advantage and words over the floor; the vocabulary is what writing through rows does to a residual stream.
- `e557 → e557b`: conservation across scale and data at 0.40, then the ceiling within a run (0.55) and the rows' forgetting of their initialization (0.071); the words outlive their rows.
- `e558 → e558b`: the transplant and the merge, then the rise split: the direction is spoken before the row arrives (session 95), the row turns into it, and it leaves when the cloud erodes (session 90).
- `e559, e560`: the ledger asked to do what no state does; the nulls are the states', the novelty lead is real.
- `e561 → e561b`, `e564 → e564b`: the instrument's screen timed alone; the fine-tuning result read against noise of matched damage, which retires word-set Jaccard as a stability statistic.
