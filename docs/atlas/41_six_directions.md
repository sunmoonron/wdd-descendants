# 41. Six directions beyond the descendants (S110)

**Question.** After the paper, six directions the record had not tested: whether recruitment matters for performance, an unlearning audit with provenance, recruitment-guided pruning, twin-based stitching, superposition counts, and the instrument's own magnitude bias. Each with its controls; four are nulls.

**Established.**
- Recruitment is not a performance event: aligned on it the class's loss drops -0.1231 and token-matched positions outside every class drop -0.1380 (difference -0.0102 [-0.0292, +0.0116]; OLMo-1B -0.0111 [-0.1104, +0.0512]); the row's direction removed from the class costs 0.0047 nats two checkpoints before recruitment and 0.0109 two after (a random direction 0.0002). What recruitment dates is the assignment of a parameter to a direction the network already uses (e591).
- Bounded unlearning leaves the rows that wrote the forgotten classes untouched and silences them there: at +2 nats of forget loss the forget words' rows keep cosine 1.0000 and change 0.89 times as much as rows of the same norm, while 0.47 of them still write at their classes (from 1.00; retain words 0.96) and blocks 0-6 carry 0.83 of the KL split; the share still writing tracks relearnability at Spearman 0.71 (e592b; e592 ran away and is kept as a killed protocol).
- Pruning helps grokking whatever is pruned (3000 / 3000 / 3500 against 4000), and S ranks the eventual words below average until generalisation (0.62 of them in the lowest half at 1500, 0.69 at 3500) (e593, e593c). Twins are not privileged stitching anchors (e594). Classes per word rise from 0.15 to 3.53 through training at K = 512, the level depending on K (e595). The magnitude bias is the support's: the oracle support removes it, no refit does (e596).

## How the results flow

- `e585, e587, e589 → e591 → e591b`: the row is recruited by a drift toward a class the states already hold; here whether the class's loss or the direction's effect changes at recruitment. Neither does; e591b adds the controls that say what the direction's cost is relative to, and e591c matches the controls on difficulty.
- `e592 → e592b`: the first protocol ran away; the bounded one gives the audit.
- `e586, e567 → e593 → e593c`: the blocked recruit and the late association of S with the words; here S as a pruning guide at 1500, then at 3000 and 3500.
- `e557, e566 → e594`: twins across runs and families; here as anchors for a stitch.
- `e587, e588 → e595`: row-free clusters as the class's definition; here counted per word and per cluster.
- The ICLR paper's magnitude bias `→ e596`.
