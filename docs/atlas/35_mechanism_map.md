# 35. Theory extraction: the arrows tested and the mechanism map (S102)

**Question.** After session 101 the question above the program became "why does a trained network organise its computation into persistent associations between native write directions and classes of its state distribution". The next artifact was to be a mechanism map with every arrow annotated rather than another experiment; the arrows that were open were tested first (eight experiments with four follow-ups), then the map was written ([`MECHANISM_MAP.md`](../MECHANISM_MAP.md)).

**Established.**
- The partition of the state space into context classes is the data's: independently seeded Pythia-160m runs twin by context sets at 0.34-0.38 (null 0.03), Pythia-410m twins with Mamba-130m at 0.46 and with OLMo-1B at 0.41 by character spans (e566); the "weight-seed" and "data-seed" variants start from main's initialisation whatever their uploaded step 0 says (e566d).
- The loop runs from the data to the neuron: at one-checkpoint resolution the row's part of an entrant's rise is +0.045 against the cloud's +0.032, the row turns toward the cloud's variance (1.035 against a random turn's 0.970) and the cloud does not grow along the row (e571); what a later neuron carries into an earlier model is its write direction (0.54 of entrants become words) and not its norm (0.18) or its input weights (0.18) (e565).
- The coalition is in Mamba, stronger than in Pythia (within-row 0.857 against 0.000 across rows), with the own row first and the near misses as selective as the words (e570).
- In the toy the association tracks generalisation and nothing else: every knob that keeps the run generalising keeps the words at 1.8-2.5 times the rotated words' S; every knob that makes it memorise removes it (e567).
- A word is a large row that the cloud makes extreme at a class of contexts the data fixes: norm-matched non-word rows describe the states equally at K=8 (e569b) but are used 0 times against 19, sit at S 0.92 against 1.61, and twin across models at 0.02 against 0.40 (e572).
- Stability has levels, row identity > usage > function > membership (e569); the invented-name lead is a generic construction row with no causal role for its own write, the ledger separating known entity from name-like string (e568).

## How the results flow

- `e566 → e566b → e566c → e566d`: the partition's convergence, then three steps to learn what the Pythia seed variants share; the independent seeds are the test, and they twin at 0.34-0.38.
- `e558b → e571`, `e558 → e565`: the rise at one-checkpoint resolution and the factorial transplant together give the loop's direction: the row turns into what the cloud speaks, and the direction is what travels.
- `e569 → e569b → e572`: the hierarchy, its baseline that retired the description level as a property of the words, and the re-statement of what is the words'.
- `e563 → e570`, `e556 → e567`, `e560 → e568`: the static mechanism on Mamba, the learning-dynamics sweep, the invented-name lead closed.
- All of it → the mechanism map, with the open arrows listed there.
