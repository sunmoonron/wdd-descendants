# 24. The boundary's carrier: the counterfactual chord, the criterion across texts, the consensus of parts, the sources (S73-S77)

**Question.** e516b found that a row's largest projection over the extreme-value floor predicts its entry into the vocabulary at the next checkpoint. What carries that projection, the position's co-written chord or the rest of the state, and does the criterion hold across texts and models?

**Established.**
- The chord does not carry it: with the position's 64 largest writes replaced by random rows of the same blocks, permuted among themselves, or removed, at every position and with the floor recomputed, the prospective prediction of entry at the next checkpoint is within 0.04 of the real state's (Pythia block 12, origins 2000-32000: real 0.86-0.97, fake 0.85-0.95, permuted 0.86-0.97, none 0.86-0.96), and removing the row's own write costs 0.01-0.05; the projection that predicts wordhood is carried by the embeddings, attention's output and the crowd of small writes (e517, e517b).
- The entrants rise because their projections rise: from 0.135 to 0.215 of the state norm over training, against a floor that moves from 0.159 to 0.145; the projection's share of the rise is 0.80-1.14 in log terms at every origin (e517b).
- Across texts at the end of training the criterion picks a disjoint text set's new words at 0.86-0.91 in GPT-2, 0.95-0.97 in Pythia and 0.90-0.93 in OLMo, level with usage and ahead of magnitude except in OLMo, where the words are the large rows and magnitude nearly matches (e518).

- No part of the state supplies the prediction alone: split exactly into centred parts, attention's ranks the next checkpoint's entrants at 0.56-0.68 (Pythia block 12), the crowd's at 0.69-0.79, the chord's at 0.69-0.89, all the MLP writes' at 0.78-0.86, the whole state's at 0.86-0.97; each part alone clears the floor for most rows (attention 0.965, the crowd 0.927) while the whole does for 0.095, so the parts cancel along most atoms and the words are the atoms along which they add; the carrier at the entrants' maxima moves from the crowd (0.85 at step 512) to attention (0.4) and the chord (0.25), the entrants not writing at their own maximum until late (e519, e519b).

- Given its size, the composition of a row's projection adds nothing: the agreement index, the constructive fraction and the sign consensus of the parts at the row's best position read the next checkpoint's entrants at 0.35-0.63 against 0.75-0.97 for the size, are at chance within deciles of the size (0.34-0.61), and rows near the floor enter at the same rate whether their parts agree or cancel; the criterion is on the resultant alone (e520).

- The alignment is made locally and diffusely: by first-order transport validated by ablation (1.02-1.05), a future word's projection at its best position is supplied 0.62-0.75 by the MLP writes at that position (0.55-0.73 the write vectors themselves), 0.14-0.24 by attention's outputs, 0.00-0.06 by writes at other positions; current words supply 0.02-0.09, the five largest sources are non-words from blocks below, and the participation ratio of the sources is 30,000-120,000 writes; size-matched non-entrants have the same source structure, and the unembedding's directions do not separate them (AUC 0.47-0.61) (e521).
- Counted causally, the sources are a few thousand writes, not a hundred thousand: the absolute attribution mass is 30-67 times the projection, so the participation ratio measured cancellation; ablating the top 10/100/1000/10,000 attributed writes removes 0.09-0.18/0.25-0.30/0.51-0.57/0.89-0.92 of an entrant's projection (first-order 0.08-0.17/0.22-0.27/0.35-0.40/0.46-0.65), the largest single write 0.03-0.08, ten thousand random writes at the position 0.16-0.31; matched non-entrants have the same curve (e521b).

## How the results flow

- `e516b → e517, e517b`. The criterion's carrier: not the chord of e505-e511, which the counterfactual removes without loss, but the state's other content.
- `e521 → e521b`. The participation ratio counted; the carriers are thousands and the ratio was cancellation.
- `e520 → e521`. With composition closed, the sources by transport: the same position's mass of small writes, not the vocabulary, not descendants from elsewhere.
- `e519b → e520`. The consensus reading tested directly: the agreement of the parts is a function of the size and selects nothing among resultants of one size.
- `e517b → e519, e519b`. What e517b left: which part carries the projection. None; the parts are redundant for the ranking and cancel along most atoms, the words being the atoms along which they agree.
- `e516b → e518`. The criterion across texts and models, the substitute for checkpoints where there are none.
