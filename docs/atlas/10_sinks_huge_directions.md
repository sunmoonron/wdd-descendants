# 10. Sinks, massive activations and the huge directions M

**Question.** What are the sink positions and the few huge directions of the state, and which of the program's claims did they distort?
**Established.** Phase-1 sink states (norm above 10 times the median) are essentially one neuron's write on one massive channel, removed by the last blocks in every model (e20, e55). The sink-contaminated centring mean puts the sink atom first in 38-60% of typical supports in SmolLM2 and OLMo, yet typical-only centring leaves identification unchanged, and the massive channels do not explain GPT-2's unreadability (e120, e126, e170). Phase 3 never excluded sinks, and session 44 traced its largest numbers to them: Pythia's 86% of the variance in the top-8 directions M was one `"\n\n\n"` token per sequence, OLMo's 48% one outlier and Qwen2.5-7B's 97% one `" series"` token; at ordinary positions M holds 10-26% (11% at 7B) in the same subspace (e432, e437b). The phase-3 false friends were mis-described sinks, while self-description and the replacement model's compounding are unchanged (e437, e392b). Correctly measured, M is an early-written channel carrying token identity, and removing it costs 2-4 times its quadratic prediction, a knee with no single seat (e433, e438, e441).
**Start here:** e20, e126, e432, e437, e441 · **Sessions:** S1, S2-3, S44 · **Scripts:** `scripts/e20_sinks.py`, `scripts/e432_huge_direction_anatomy.py`, `scripts/e437_sink_hygiene.py`, `scripts/e441_knee_decomposition.py`; the rest are `scripts/<id>_*.py`

## How the results flow

- `e01 → e20 → e55`: e01 saw the massive channel vanish at the last level; e20 finds each sink is one neuron's write (GPT-2 mlp2#1825 on channel 447), and e55 finds what removes it: attention in GPT-2, MLPs in SmolLM2 and Qwen.
- `e20 → e89 → e126 / e120`: the audit flagged that the cached centring mean includes sink states. The mean holds SmolLM2's sink neuron (e89) and the sink atom tops many typical supports (e126), but typical-only centring barely moves the gap (e120), so the contamination hypothesis (H10) was killed and kept as a caveat.
- `e20 → e170`: removing the five massive channels leaves increment recall unchanged, so GPT-2's within-block unreadability is coherence and within-block cancellation, not the channel-447 dynamics.
- `e400, e404, e418, e426, e392 → e432` (why a correction was needed): phase 1 dropped positions above 10 times the median norm and phase 3 kept them. Phase 3 then reported that M holds 0.86 of Pythia's variance with 0.015 of its Fisher trace (e400), 0.48 of OLMo's (e426) and 0.97 at 7B (e418), that false friends act through M (e404), and that the replacement model compounds worst in Pythia and OLMo (e392). The session-44 survey's most likely pick (p 0.45) was this confound.
- `e432 → e437 → e437b → e392b` (the correction chain): e432 finds one `"\n\n\n"` per Pythia sequence at 41x the median norm holding 0.83 of the variance and one OLMo `" don"` at 46x holding 0.44, while at typical positions M holds 0.10-0.26 in all five, in the same subspace (overlap 0.87-0.88), and replacing the sinks by the mean state costs nothing. e437 shows the false friends were mis-described sinks, own words are unchanged, and Pythia's Fisher per unit variance is 0.09, not 0.017. e437b finds the 7B 0.97 was one `" series"` token at 221x (M holds 0.11, so e419's 29% of the loss comes from 11% of the variance). e392b finds the compounding is not a sink effect.
- `e432 → e433`: e432 finds M carried rather than repaired (the next block keeps 0.93-1.14 of a halving along M, OLMo 0.70, against 0.52-0.76 along random directions) and kills the set point (odd part 16-29x the even part), normalisation ballast (a norm-only change costs at most +0.010) and the temperature knob (output norm within 5%); e433 finds M written early (0.65-0.91 predictable from the state after block 0) and identity-heavy (position too in GPT-2), its token part costing 2.4-6.6x more per unit energy.
- `e432 → e433 → e438 → e441`: the knee (removal costs 2.0-3.8x its quadratic prediction) is not the size of the move (a matched random move stays near quadratic, 1.1-1.3), not identity decoding (e433), not the attention softmax (e438), and only partly MLP gating (e441).
