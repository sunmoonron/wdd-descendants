# 25. Training's hand: the gradient at the origin, the rows' motion, the breadth of a word, the time course (S77-S78)

**Question.** e521 found that a future word's alignment is made at its position by a mass of small writes. What does training do to make it: does the loss gradient at the origin pull the state or the row onto the direction, does the state move onto the row or the row onto the state, and where do the rows that become words move?

**Established.**
- The gradient at the origin does not select the entrants: a one-step move along the negative gradient of the loss (the flow; the sign of the gradient as the Adam-like direction; 12k and 65k held-out tokens; the step linear, ratio 2.00 when doubled) changes S in a way that reads the next checkpoint's entrants at 0.47-0.54 over all non-words, 0.48-0.55 within deciles of S and 0.46-0.64 against S-matched non-entrants, with Spearman 0.00-0.02 against the actual change of S; the local pulls at 0.45-0.59. The flow's prediction agrees between halves of the gradient text at Spearman 0.19-0.44, so the null stands for its reliable part; the row's own gradient direction agrees at 0.02-0.05 and has cosine 0.002 with the row's actual change, so it cannot be estimated from 65k tokens and its null is uninformative (e522, e522b).
- The rise of an entrant's S is the row moving, not the states: with the next checkpoint's rows against the origin's states the rise is 0.64-0.78 of the actual rise at block 12 (0.56-0.68 at block 6); with the next states against the origin's rows 0.02-0.24; matched non-entrants fall by 0.04-0.13. Every row moves by a similar angle (cosine 0.82-0.88, relative change 0.6-0.9 per interval) and grows (norm ratio 1.19-1.57, entrants slightly more); one gradient step moves the rows by 1e-5 of their norm against 2-3e-3 for the states, so the rows' motion is the accumulated update (e522).
- Where the entrants' rows move: into the state cloud's principal subspace (the change's fraction in the top-32 components 0.048-0.063 against 0.030-0.040 for non-words and 0.031 isotropic; AUC against matched 0.66-0.78) and toward the state at their best position (cosine 0.085-0.121 against 0.022-0.073 matched, 0.006-0.015 non-words, 0.00 at random positions; positive for 79-94% of entrants), about half of it outside the top-32 components (residual 0.037-0.069 against 0.011-0.044); the best position persists for only 38-60% of entrants, the rise at the old position (0.01-0.08) a fraction of the rise of the maximum (0.09-0.15); the words lie twice as much in the top-32 components as non-words (0.082 against 0.037-0.049) (e523).
- Wordhood has a breadth criterion beyond the maximum: within deciles of S the count of positions over the floor reads the entrants at 0.71-0.85, the mean squared projection over positions at 0.68-0.77 and the row's fraction in the top-32 components at 0.64-0.71 (e523; e516b had the rate of positions over the floor just below the maximum unconditionally).
- In time, at a thousand steps: entry is a directed drift of five thousand steps or more (consecutive increments at cosine 0.28-0.31 against 0.24-0.25 for matched rows and 0.15 falling to negative values for the typical row), in which the row's fraction in the top-32 subspace rises at every checkpoint and its breadth rises steadily (13 to 18 positions over three quarters of the floor against 9-10) while the maximum barely moves (1.02 to 1.07), then a crossing of two thousand steps (the maximum 1.07 to 1.25, positions over the floor 1 to 7, breadth to 33, the pull toward the best state doubled relative to matched rows, 0.090 against 0.043, AUC 0.70-0.73), then a plateau with the increments decorrelating; the row's own motion keeps its profile (Spearman 0.96-0.99) and its old best position first, the states' motion moving the maximum to rank 2-4 (e524, e524b).

## How the results flow

- `e521 → e522, e522b`. The sources known, the question moves to training: the gradient at the origin, tested by a one-step move and controlled for its own noise. It does not select; the row moves.
- `e523 → e524, e524b`. The endpoint motion resolved in time: a directed drift with breadth rising first, a fast crossing, a plateau; the row's motion keeps the profile, the states' moves the maximum.
- `e522 → e523`. If the row moves, where: into the cloud's principal subspace and toward the states where it already projects most, with the maximum not defended at one position; and the breadth criterion that falls out.
