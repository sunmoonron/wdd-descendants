# 77. Switches from the weights: token switches by table lookup at a weights-set dose, a persona's persistence from one steered token, two rows at once, the word's own direction against the row, and the fifty-row ablation (S169)

**Question.** Session 168 left the direct-path table as a label and not a finder. Asked the other way round, which row says this token, the table might hand out controls rather than personas: one lookup, one dose rule from the weights, one count, no judge. Can a control over the output be found by asking the direct-path table which row says a token and poking that row at a dose set from the weights, with no search, no judge and no training (S1 to S7: the answer's end, the opener, Chinese, capitals, the smiley, the model's name, markdown; T1 the think token); does a zoo persona survive when the injection covers only the prompt and the first k generated tokens, and does the row fire on its own text read back with no injection (P1 to P3); do two zoo rows at once carry both themes (M1 to M3); does the theme's own output direction do what the row does (W1 to W3); do the fifty rows with the largest read energy hold the model up (A1 to A3); and where are the one-token rows (C1)?

**Established.**
- switches from the weights: a table lookup plus one dose rule hands out two clean controls, the model's name (suppressed, 20 of 20 answers rename the model Q3 and 14 of 20 say Baidu, at 0.31 nats) and the end-of-turn row (answers 364 to 11 tokens at one sign, every answer to the cap at the other), while the rows found for Chinese, capitals, markdown and the think token fire and then loop and no emoji row exists (2 of 7 switches held as written); a persona survives its own injection, the prompt plus one generated token keeping the theme for 4 of 6 zoo rows and 16 tokens for 6 of 6, the row firing at 5 to 22 times its base level on its own answers; two rows at once let the louder persona take over while word-themes coexist; the word's own direction equals or beats the row for heavy and cat at a third of the damage and fails for the four persona themes; zeroing the fifty largest-read-energy rows costs 0.015 nats: reading: a lookup in the direct-path table plus one dose rule from the weights hands out two clean controls and a drawer of spammers. The row whose direct path says the 'wen' of the model's name, suppressed at layer 27, renames the model Q3 in 20 of 20 answers and makes 14 of them say Baidu, fully coherent at 0.31 nats (0.11 nats at the smoke's lower dose, same flip); the row whose direct path is the end-of-turn token cuts answers from 364 tokens to 11 at one sign (15 of 20 non-degenerate, the rest one-line) and runs all 20 to the cap at the other (the base has 15 of 20 there, which is why the lengthening bar, set at 1.4 times the base, could not be met). The rows found for Chinese, capitals, markdown and the think token all do what their token says and then loop on it: the Chinese row fills 80 percent of the characters with a two-character phrase (19 of 20 answers loop by a character check the word-level check missed, so S3 is a hollow hold), the capitals row shouts a readable sentence (9 of 20 answers both shouting and clean over their first 40 words) before looping, the markdown row at its suppressing sign removes a third of the bold markers and at its pushing sign emits 392 asterisks, the think row emits only think tags. No row says the smiley. At 4 and 8 nats of direct-path shift the last-layer rows move nothing (KL 0.005 to 0.08), at 16 the one-token rows spam, so the direct path cannot dose a row whose vocabulary is one token; by the pivot rule the dose has to be measured, not read. The segues carry the session. A persona survives its own injection: with the zoo's 0.8 on the prompt and one generated token only, 4 of 6 zoo rows keep their theme at half the all-token rate or better (Galactic 9 of 10, Vegas 7 of 10), and with 16 tokens all six do; read back on its own all-token answers with no injection, each row fires at 5 to 22 times its level on the base answers and at 0.5 to 1.8 times its natural maximum, so the write side of the atom feeds its read side through the text it wrote. Two rows at once: Jesus silences 4 of 5 partners, Vegas silences Galactic, and Heavy, a word-theme, blends with 4 of 5 (both themes in 6 to 9 of 10 answers), 12 of 15 pairs intact. The word's own output direction, dosed by the same rule, matches the row for heavy (10 of 10 at 0.39 nats against the row's 0.84) and beats it for cat (5 of 10 at 0.11 against 1 of 10 at 0.45) and reaches 1 to 4 of 10 for the four persona themes against the rows' 7 to 10: the vocabulary is the library for words and the rows are the library for personas, as session 168 read it. Zeroing the fifty rows with the largest R_nat costs 0.015 nats, fifty random rows 0.001: no such fifty hold the model up (e731).
