# 09. The quotient program (S31)

**Question.** Is the quotient (the low-dimensional, causally relevant part of the perturbation response) a structured object or only a compressible one?
**Established.** In all five models one quotient serves every observable and perturbation family tried (e317, e335, e342); its dimension, 8-32, is set by the map (e319, e329); it is additive and homogeneous in the coordinate (e327, e337), linear to 3-10x the natural amplitude (e345), read and damped by the later MLPs and spread across tokens only by attention (e341, e351, e352). The decisive results are negative: a quotient fitted to shuffled targets scores the same (e340), PCA equals PLS (e347), the discarded complement decodes as well (e346), there is no null space, coset or fibre structure (e336, e338), and WDD atoms are not a sparse, concentrated or semantic basis of it, a block's coordinate living in the ledger's small-coefficient tail (e332-e334). The response cloud is low-rank and every moderate-dimensional high-variance projection carries the same causal information: compressible, not structured. Phase 2 found the core results hold at four depths (e361) and the coordinate is not conserved in training (e365b).
**Start here:** e317, e340, e346, e342, e333 · **Sessions:** S31 · **Scripts:** `scripts/e317_*.py` to `scripts/e356_*.py`, helper `scripts/quot_common.py`

## How the results flow

- `e288 → e317 → e335 → e340 → e347`, `e295 → e346 → e361`: one quotient serves nine observables; a basis fitted to nothing, a shuffled-target fit, PCA and the discarded complement all do as well: compressible, not structured; e361 repeats this at four depths.
- `e307 → e320 → e321`: session 27's channels persist only in a fixed basis and only in GPT-2, Pythia, OLMo; refitted bases rotate inside a persistent subspace (H110 narrowed to H128).
- `e313 → e325 → e326 → e327`, `e337 → e353`: equivalence is flat and closed under sums and scaling in the coordinate, weak at the logits; responses add, and dense superposition fails only through the total norm.
- `e302 → e329 → e330 → e345`: past the linear range the dimension collapses and responses converge (H132 killed); along core directions the response is linear to 3-10x.
- `e273 → e336`, `e311 → e338`, `e313 → e332 → e333 → e334`: no null space or cosets, random fibres; atoms are ordinary members of the cloud and a block's coordinate lives in the ledger's tail.
- `e298 → e341`, `e307 → e351 → e352`: later MLPs read the coordinate (4/5) and damp the image; attention is the only cross-token route.
- `e291 → e349 → e365 → e365b, e373`: born by step 4000; phase 2 dates it to the induction transition (steps 512-1000) and finds no persistence through training.
- `e264 → e339 → e356 → e360d`: two thirds of the coordinate is implicit in state or source, yet a direction's coordinate varies across contexts unpredictably; e360d is the training-time form.
