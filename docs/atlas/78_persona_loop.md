# 78. The persona loop: cutting the row after a steered prefix, amplifying the row's own write, rewiring a listener's write to a persona, and exporting the prefix to another model (S170)

**Question.** Session 169 found that a zoo persona survives when the injection covers only the prompt and the first tokens, and that the row then fires on its own words. Where does the persona live after the prefix: in the row (A, zero the row's write from token 17 on), in the words (D, hand the 16-token prefix to a second model; e732b, re-prefill it as text in the same model) or in the steered cache; can the row's own natural firing ignite the persona with no injection when its write column is multiplied by a gain of 10 to 1000 (B); and can a persona be wired to a topic by one column edit, keeping a topic-selective neuron's read side and overwriting its write with the card's direction scaled to deliver the zoo's dose at that neuron's own on-topic activation (C)?

**Established.**
- the persona loop: after a 16-token steered prefix the row is not where the persona lives (zeroing it from token 17 on leaves the continuation hit rates unchanged, 0.2, 0.5, 0.3, 0.8, 1.0, 0.1 against 0.2, 0.5, 0.4, 0.8, 1.0, 0.2) and the words are only part of it (the same prefixes re-prefilled as text give 0.2, 0.0, 0.3, 0.3, 0.7, 0.2 and a second model 0.0, 0.2, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.1), so the steered cache carries the rest; the row's own firing ignites its persona only at 300 times its natural write and only for three rows (Jesus, Vegas, Heavy at 4 to 5 of 10 unrelated answers, text intact, 0.46 to 0.77 nats), with 1000 times breaking zoo and random rows alike; one column edit wires the Jesus persona to the identity prompts (20 of 20 identity answers, 0 of 20 generic, 0.40 nats, the control listener leaking onto 12 of 20 generic answers) and the Heavy persona with a leak (20 of 20 and 6 of 20), while the four other cards miss: reading: after the prefix the persona is not in the neuron. With the zoo's injection on the prompt and the first 16 generated tokens, zeroing the row's write for the rest of the answer leaves every row's continuation where it was (Jesus 0.2, Hugs 0.5, Cozy 0.3, Galactic 0.8, Vegas 1.0, Heavy 0.1 against 0.2, 0.5, 0.4, 0.8, 1.0, 0.2; a random row zeroed gives the same), so session 169's self-hearing, the row firing at 5 to 22 times its base level on its own words, is a reading of the text and not the loop that keeps it going. Nor is it only in the words: the same 16-token prefixes re-prefilled as text with no injection continue the theme at 0.2, 0.0, 0.3, 0.3, 0.7, 0.2, and a second model (Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507) at 0.0, 0.2, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.1, below the steered-cache continuation for the three strong rows (Hugs 0.5, Galactic 0.8, Vegas 1.0) and level with it for the weak ones; the keys and values computed under the injection carry the rest, which is the cache hypothesis of the persona-vector literature measured on single rows. The row's own firing can ignite its persona, but only when its write is multiplied by about 300 and only for three rows: Jesus, Vegas and Heavy reach 4 to 5 of 10 unrelated answers with the text intact at 0.46 to 0.77 nats, half the cost of the zoo's injection, while Hugs, Cozy and Galactic stay at 0 to 1 of 10; at 10 to 100 x nothing moves (KL at most 0.22), and at 1000 x the zoo rows and six random rows are damaged alike (2.4 to 4.0 nats, 2 to 8 of 10 answers degenerate) with the random rows never hitting the paired regex. One column edit can wire a persona to a topic: the layer-15 row most selective for the identity answers (6 times its generic activation), with its read side kept and its write replaced by the Jesus direction scaled to the zoo's dose at its own identity activation, puts the persona on 20 of 20 identity answers and 0 of 20 generic ones, every answer coherent, at 0.40 nats, where a listener with no selectivity puts it on 20 of 20 and 12 of 20 with half the answers degenerate at 1.56 nats; the layer-24 listener does the same for Heavy with a leak (20 of 20 and 6 of 20, 0.27 nats); the layer-4 and layer-5 listeners deliver too little for Vegas, Hugs, Cozy and Galactic (0 to 6 of 20 identity answers), so the recipe exists and is not yet general: the scale comes from the listener's base-model activation and the edited model's answers may not fire it as hard. Six of fourteen statements held and all six are controls or nulls, which is the honest shape of the session: the positives are single rows, the neuron's job ends at the first words, and what carries the persona afterwards is the cache and the words together (e732, e732b).

## How the results flow

- `77 e731 → e732`: e731's persistence from one steered token and the row's self-hearing are the loop; e732 cuts the row, amplifies it, rewires it and exports the prefix.
- `74 e728 → e732`: e728's six strong zoo rows, regexes, base answers and median residual norms; e731's identity and generic answers choose the listeners.
- `e732 → e733`: the persona after the prefix is in the cache and the words; the cache transplant layer by layer is the next mechanistic step, and the listener's scale from the edited model is the next recipe step.
