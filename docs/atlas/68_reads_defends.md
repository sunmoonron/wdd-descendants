# 68. What the model reads and defends (S160)

**Question.** Both camps of the debate are object-first and reader-blind: one counts neurons, the other counts features, and both grade a unit by whether a human can read it and a decomposition by how much of the state it reconstructs. What are the model's own criteria? What its readers read at a position (e707), whether the crowd is a loop (e708), the time-scales of its coordinates (e709), whether the gated remainder is a branch of a program (e710), what training defends (e711), and whether the ledger's edges form a finite program (e712).

**Established.**
- The read subspace: the output's Fisher quadratic form predicts the finite KL of removing a part at Spearman 0.83 to 0.92, reads the weak write at 0.72 to 1.41 times a random direction and the strong write at 0.70 to 0.99, the weak field at 1.23 to 2.51, and an SAE's error on GPT-2 small at 1.38 to 1.53, its share of the read energy 0.14 to 0.14 against 0.15 to 0.19 of the energy: reading: the model grades a state by a quadratic form, not a subspace: the Fisher information of its output over the next sixty-four tokens predicts the finite KL of removing any part of a position's state at Spearman 0.83 to 0.92, where every subspace cut from the same Fisher failed finite removals in the smokes (LayerNorm couples the parts; see the revisions). Graded per unit of energy, parts are not graded by their energy: GPT-2's sixteen native OMP rows are its most-read part and its own strong write the least, in Pythia and Qwen every part is read at about its energy, the public SAE's error on GPT-2 small holds the same share of the content's read energy as of its Euclidean energy and costs 1.7 to 3 times a random vector of its norm to remove (Gurnee's pathology as read energy: the dark matter is read like the rest), and the fields are read at about twice a random direction, below the pre-registered three, with the stream's leading directions far above. Neither camp's currency is privileged by the output: the rows pass above their energy in GPT-2 only, the dark matter at its energy, the collective field as a second-class citizen. (e707).
- The elasticity test: the expected gradient points back toward rotated write rows with a cosine of 0.10 to 0.14 (all the block's strong rows 0.09 to 0.12, the swapped field 0.09 to 0.17, a symmetry -0.01 to 0.00), training returns the rows by 0.01 to 0.02 in 200 steps, and the swapped field's loss recovers by 0.92 to 1.09 with its writers free and 0.93 to 1.11 frozen while the field stays swapped: reading: training defends the function, not the object: on the training distribution the expected gradient at the perturbed point points back toward the original rows with a cosine of 0.09 to 0.14, three to five times the noise floor, the same for sixteen strong rows, sixteen weak rows, all the block's strong rows and the swapped weak field (0.09 to 0.17 on its plane), with a force no larger than an unperturbed row's; the rotations cost 0.0003 to 0.02 nats, and in 200 steps at a realistic rate the rows return by 0.01 to 0.02 while the restoring cosine fades to the floor. The swapped field decides it: a third of a nat, recovered by 0.92 to 1.09 with the writers free and 0.93 to 1.11 with them frozen, while the writers restore the field by 0.02, the readers' energy in the swapped subspace does not move and the recomputed field stays swapped (0.67 to 0.72). The network routes around the displacement in other weights and stops feeling it: a unit survives by what it does, not by where it is. (e711).
- Branches: one linear bypass per branch of the open set recovers 0.59 to 0.66 of the zeroing cost in the gated models against 0.58 to 0.72 for a single linear bypass, 0.54 to 0.65 for clusters of the input and 0.67 to 0.80 for the oracle: reading: the gated remainder is not control flow either: one linear bypass per branch of the open set, sixteen branches found on the gate's sign pattern, recovers no more of the zeroing cost in the gated models than the single linear bypass, four branches do exactly as well as one, sixteen clusters of the input do worse, the branches transfer out of domain no better, and the GELU models gain 0.03. After e705's random quadratic and these branches, what the single linear map misses in a gated block is neither a low-order function of the input nor a branch indexed by the open set or by the input's clusters: it is position-specific in a way the block's own control signal does not index, which leaves it to the attention that wrote the input. (e710).
- The thermostat: a block's strong writes scaled by one and a half are undone along their own direction by 0.34 to 0.72 over the next three blocks, 0.06 to 0.13 of it by the weak writes and 0.28 to 0.58 by the strong, while the output KL is 0.03 to 272.59 times the direct path's: reading: the crowd is not a thermostat: when a block's strong writes are scaled by one and a half, the next three blocks undo a third to three quarters of the injection along its own direction, but the strong writes do the undoing (0.28 to 0.58) and the weak writes a tenth of it (0.06 to 0.13), attention adds nothing, and the response is the same at a half and at double (linear, no brake against excess); the stream's displacement after three blocks is nine tenths of the injection, in other directions. What the later blocks make of it depends on the family: in Pythia, OLMo and Qwen the output KL of the perturbation is six to several hundred times its direct path's (amplification), in GPT-2 medium a third and in SmolLM2-135M a thirtieth (repair). Either way the agent is the strong writes, not the crowd: the crowd's job is the brake off the class (e697), not a loop across depth. (e708).
- Two clocks: the weak field's coordinates have a time-scale of 1.62 to 25.57 tokens against 1.83 to 18.81 for the most active neurons and 7.81 to 57.66 for the stream's principal directions, and jump 0.94 to 1.13 times more across a document boundary than inside one: reading: there are no two clocks: every coordinate decorrelates within a token or two, the weak field's integral time-scale (1.6 to 26 tokens) is not systematically longer than the most active neurons' (1.8 to 19), three times longer at two Pythia-410m blocks and shorter in GPT-2 medium, the strong field is as slow or slower, and the stream's own sixteen principal directions are the slowest at six blocks of eight; across a document boundary no coordinate jumps more than inside a document. The field is a second kind of atom for the readers (area 66), not a second time-scale: whatever slow state the model keeps lives in the stream's leading directions, which both fields leave alone. (e709).
- The edge program: four fifths of a firing's net drive needs 2 to 12 MLP units, a neuron's stable wires hold 0.00 to 0.17 of its wire mass, the top tenth of active edges carries 0.49 to 0.64 of the mass with a Zipf slope of 0.57 to 0.81, and the top hundredth of upstream units holds 1.00 to 1.00 of the stable edges: reading: at the wire level the model is not a finite program of reused edges: four fifths of a firing's net drive needs two to twelve MLP units (the net ledger's concentration, e695) where its positive drive needs thousands, but they are different units at different firings: a neuron's sixteen-input wires span 900 to 1300 distinct units over 163 firings, in Pythia and Qwen no unit is a wire at half of them (in GPT-2 medium four such wires hold 0.17 of the mass), and the edge mass has a Zipf slope of 0.6 to 0.8, flatter than a program's vocabulary. The units are stable objects; the chord is composed anew at each firing. (e712).

## How the results flow

- `66 e703; 67 e706 → e707`: the dictionary's parts scored not by energy but inside the subspace the output reads at each position.
- `65 e697; 66 e701 → e708`: the crowd tested as a loop, a block's strong writes scaled and the next blocks' responses read with the ledger.
- `66 e704; 67 e705 → e709`: the field's coordinates timed along the sequence against the neurons' and the stream's.
- `67 e705; 65 e696 → e710`: the gated remainder read as control flow, one bypass per branch of the block's open set.
- `65 e701; 66 e702 → e711`: unit-hood as what training defends, rows and the field perturbed and training continued.
- `64 e695; 65 e697 → e712`: the ledger's wires counted as edges across positions and neurons.
