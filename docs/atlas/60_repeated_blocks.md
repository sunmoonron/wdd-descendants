# 60. Repeating a block on small models (S143)

**Question.** Ng's RYS runs a contiguous block of middle layers a second time with no weight change and reports gains on hard probes at 72B. At 135M to 500M parameters, where does a repeat help or hurt, is the gain knowledge or format, what does the second traversal do to the first's writes, and can WDD predict, replace or repair the repeat?

**Established.**
- Repeating a block of 1 to 8 layers (162, 126, 126 blocks in SmolLM2-135M, Pythia-410m, Qwen2.5-0.5B) never lowers the wikitext-2 loss: the least damaging repeat costs +0.050 nats (14-15 of 30), +0.057 nats (8-9 of 24), +0.045 nats (9-11 of 24), and the worst +8.20 (early blocks), +3.23 (late blocks), +2.14 (late blocks); on the probes the block chosen on half the items raises accuracy on the other half by +0.16 (10-13; +0.25 with the function words masked), -0.06 (6-7; -0.05 with the function words masked), +0.31 (7-11; +0.34 with the function words masked), the gain carried by e560's capitals (+0.32, +0.01, +0.59 on the held-out block) while addition, weekdays and months stay flat or fall; across blocks the 16-word support Jaccard predicts the damage worse than the plain centred cosine in all three models, and the weights-only reader compatibility is the best predictor only in Pythia-410m (Spearman with minus the loss change within block size, SmolLM2-135M, Pythia-410m, Qwen2.5-0.5B: centred cosine +0.77/+0.28/+0.87, log norm ratio -0.83/-0.02/-0.82, 16-word support Jaccard +0.51/+0.02/+0.78, reader compatibility +0.03/+0.48/+0.12); at the junction of the four most damaging blocks (plain repeat +7.90, +2.84, +2.08 nats) the best fix is norm matching in SmolLM2-135M (+2.54), the rotated chord in Pythia-410m (+1.02), stripping the band's MLP writes in Qwen2.5-0.5B (+0.61), the native chord gives +9.07, +2.25, +2.05 and the rotated chord +3.20, +1.02, +1.01, and on the least damaging blocks no junction improves on the plain repeat on average (e656).
- The capitals gain is mostly format and partly ranking: the base's wrong answers are mostly function words in SmolLM2-135M (137 of 141) and quiz blanks in Qwen2.5-0.5B (55 of 115), and the repeat replaces them with a city; read under a content mask (tokens with a letter that are not function words) the held-out block still beats the base (SmolLM2-135M 0.394 to 0.831, Pythia-410m 0.681 to 0.694, Qwen2.5-0.5B 0.412 to 0.894), and in a forced choice among the capitals' own first tokens, where format cannot matter, it raises accuracy in SmolLM2-135M from 0.738 to 0.881 (24 fixed, 1 broken), in Pythia-410m from 0.787 to 0.787 (5 fixed, 5 broken), in Qwen2.5-0.5B from 0.869 to 0.975 (19 fixed, 2 broken), a forced-choice gain 0.44 of the raw gain in SmolLM2-135M and 0.18 of the raw gain in Qwen2.5-0.5B (e656b).
- On the least damaging blocks the second traversal largely repeats the first in all three models: its band increment points along the first's (cosine +0.31 to +0.65), each block's MLP output agrees with its first-traversal output (cosine +0.55 to +0.69, activations correlated +0.67 to +0.78), and the largest writes keep their sign (0.01 to 0.14 flip) while about half are replaced in the top eight (0.53 to 0.62, against 0.77 to 0.82 per loop in LOOPED.md's looped Ouro); on the most damaging blocks the second traversal computes something else (MLP output cosine +0.01 to +0.34, 0.84 to 0.95 of the top eight replaced, 0.17 to 0.43 sign flips), though the band increment still points along the first's in SmolLM2-135M's early blocks (+0.55 to +0.59) and is orthogonal to it in Qwen2.5-0.5B's last blocks (-0.06 to +0.07); Pythia-410m, the GELU model with read repulsion, cancels no more than the gated models (MLP output cosine over every block studied SmolLM2-135M +0.467, Pythia-410m +0.475, Qwen2.5-0.5B +0.320) (e658).
- Adding the band's increment again in one pass reproduces most of a helpful repeat, and the one-line raw increment does it better than the WDD chord: on Qwen2.5-0.5B's best block (7-9, true repeat +0.359 probe accuracy at +0.066 nats) half the raw increment gives +0.344 at +0.055, the energy-matched raw increment +0.347, the 16-word native chord +0.164 and the rotated chord +0.015; across the five bands per model the native chord at alpha 1 damages the loss less than the energy-matched raw increment on most bands in SmolLM2-135M, Qwen2.5-0.5B but not Pythia-410m, more than the rotated chord on most bands in every model, and recovers more probe gain than the rotated chord where there is gain to recover (SmolLM2-135M, Qwen2.5-0.5B); the chord holds 0.47, 0.30, 0.30 of the increment's energy (e657).
- Reading: repeat gains exist at 135M to 500M parameters, but only in the two gated models, only on factual recall, and mostly as format: the repeat stops the model hedging or quizzing and makes it name a city, and it also ranks the right city first a little more often. The second traversal of a friendly middle block re-runs the first's computation with the same words and signs, e509's amplification rather than LOOPED.md's erasure, which is why simply adding the band's increment again in one pass reproduces the repeat. WDD adds nothing beyond the plain baselines here: its support overlap predicts the map worse than the centred cosine in all three models (only the weights-only reader compatibility wins, and only in Pythia-410m), its chord is a worse virtual repeat than the raw increment, and its filtered junction is never the best fix.

## How the results flow

- `e656 → e656b`: the capitals gain read under a content mask and as a forced choice among capitals.
- `e656 → e657, e658`: the least damaging and best-gaining blocks, re-added in one pass and opened up.
- `22 e509, 25 e648b → e658`: amplification (the chord's own rows answer it positively) against erasure (read repulsion); amplification wins on the friendly blocks.
