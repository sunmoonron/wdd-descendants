# 07. Descendants II: function, sufficiency, capacity, dynamics (S12-S21)

**Question.** Does a write's downstream descendant carry its function, in how many dimensions, and how does it evolve?
**Established.** The descendant, not the write vector, carries a write's function: its geometry predicts logit-footprint geometry beyond write geometry, increasingly with depth (e254, e259, e260), adding the write adds nothing (e268), and injecting it reproduces the write's effect (e269). The map to the effect is near-linear with no null directions (e271, e273, e280), and the descendant, not the write, predicts its own future and effect (e281, e283). It is a compressed coordinate, not a basis: reconstruction needs the full dimension, causal observables 8-32 directions (e272, e286), and provenance rides on a coherent 16-33% of the energy at mid depth (e277). The network is not tuned to it (e276), similar descendants are not interchangeable (e262, e264), and SmolLM2's massive neuron is the one exception, a context-bound reaction carried by the block-10 MLP (e275b, e278, e284b).
**Start here:** e259, e268, e269, e277, e283 · **Sessions:** S12-S21 · **Scripts:** `scripts/e251_*.py` to `scripts/e285b_*.py`

## How the results flow

- `e254 → e255 → e259 → e260 → e268`: the bridge, tested on neighbours, under a write-matched design, across depth, then as sufficiency.
- `e262 → e264 → e383`: similar descendants are not substitutable because token-level effects are independent; e383 re-reads the KL interactions; e357, e358 confine the negative to natural-text averages.
- `e263 → e268 → e272 → e286`: dimension counts become a four-capacity ladder, which e286 cuts to two rungs (reconstruction 512-1024 vs 8-32 directions).
- `e269 → e271 → e273 → e273b → e276`: the injected descendant carries the effect linearly and near-isotropically (GPT-2's exception: its massive channel), with generic leverage.
- `e251 → e277 → e279`: dense coordinates win only for provenance; the coherent fraction explains why; late identity loss is lost coherent energy, not rotation.
- `e253 → e257 → e275b → e278 → e284b`: the massive neuron is not a within-token variable, not cross-token, off the signed law, not amplitude; one MLP carries the reaction; e290 later finds a set-point response.
- `e280 → e281 → e283`: no snapping, the law composes, the descendant predicts its future; e315 extends this to interventions (0.95-0.99).
- `e252 → e282 → e285 → e285b`: pair angles hold but gain is not isometric; e285 derives angle preservation from gain concentration; e285b leaves norm-versus-count additivity open.
