# 15. A 7B workspace agenda and established lenses (S40-S42)

**Question.** Can a state read as the model's own words surface intermediates the model computes but never states, and does that reading hold up against established lenses and across models?
**Established.** In Qwen2.5-7B, native words surface a hidden two-hop bridge entity at the subject's last token in middle blocks, where the logit lens, PCA parts and rotated words do not (e420), and the model's largest actual writes, raw or centred, rank the country first less often than the plain lens (0.15 against 0.44; e421, e421b). At the final position the bridge appears only late, together with the answer, and the plain lens reads it at least as well; roles also resolve late for every reader, and native words show the resolution as suppression of the other name (e415, e417). Self-description holds at 7B (own 0.79 against rotation 0.26 at k 16, e418), but the reported "97% of the variance in the top-8 directions" was one token, " series": at ordinary positions M holds 11% of the variance, and kept exact it carries 29% of the function (e419, corrected by e437b). On the five original models the native lens is not a better reader in general: it leads for a recalled country only in OLMo-1B (and Qwen-7B), loses to value vectors in Pythia and to the plain lens in GPT-2, and no vocabulary-space reader surfaces context tokens (e423, e424, e425). What stays new is the instrument and the property it measures, the self-description of trained networks, not a general-purpose better lens (S41-S42 readings).
**Start here:** e415, e420, e421b, e418, e424 · **Sessions:** S40, S41, S42 · **Scripts:** `scripts/e415_*.py` to `e422_*.py` with `e421b_*.py` (Qwen-7B, helper `ws_common.py`); `e423_*.py` to `e425_*.py` (five models, helper `lr_common.py`)

## How the results flow

- `e415 → e420 → e421 → e421b`. At the final position the bridge appeared only with the answer, so e420 moved the same readers to the subject's last token, where the two-hop literature places bridge resolution; native words surfaced it there (top 20 in 0.11-0.21 of prompts, against 0.00-0.01 for the lenses). e421 asked whether this is just the value-vector reading: the largest actual writes ranked the country first less often than the plain lens (0.14-0.15 against 0.31-0.44). e421b answered the fairness objection that raw-norm ranking favours massive-activation writes; centring changed nothing.
- `e415 → e420` (causal checks). At the final position, removing the bridge-carrying word costs the answer about ten times more than removing another word (0.15-0.16 against 0.01-0.02 nats), and bridge and answer sit in distinct words in 0.25-0.49 of prompts. At the subject position removal changes nothing: the information is redundant across positions.
- `e415 → e416, e417`. The same five readers on WorkspaceBench's other families. Arithmetic intermediates were beyond the model, and the native lens fabricated more digits than the plain lens. Roles resolve only after block 20 for every reader; the losing name rides on suppressing words, while the carrying words' blocks do not encode the role.
- `e418 → e419 → e437b`. Self-description holds at 7B, but a Fisher-metric pursuit made it worse. e419 kept M exact (as e404's condition B): Fisher still lost, and M alone recovered 0.29 of the loss. Session 44's e437b found the "97% of the variance" was one " series" token at 221 times the median norm in the fitting text; at ordinary positions M holds 11% of the variance and 0.4% of the Fisher trace (chance 0.2%), yet carries 29% of the function. The local metric misses M's large-displacement role.
- `e414 → e418, e419 → e422`. The most used native words point into M (e414), and at 7B the huge directions looked locally light yet recovered 29% of the loss, which suggested the super weights were the native language's function words. e422 refuted it: the super neuron's word is a huge-direction word never used after the first position.
- `e420, e421 → e421b, e424` (S42). The Qwen-7B reading advantage survives centring but, put to the five original models, holds only in OLMo-1B; value vectors win in Pythia, the plain lens in GPT-2, and SmolLM2 and Qwen-0.5B have no reader that sees the country.
- `e423 → e425`. Previous-token identity is written for key matching, not for the output vocabulary, so every reader that goes through the unembedding misses it; e425's causal test then had no previous-token word to remove.
- `e421, e422 (S41) → e421b, e423-e425 (S42) → e426`. S41 mapped most WDD results onto established names (sparse coding, value vectors, massive activations and super weights, the Hydra effect) and tested the two strongest claims directly: reading a state in the model's own vocabulary did not reduce to the actual writes (e421), and the super-weight link failed (e422). S42 put the candidate unlocks to the five original models and found no general reading advantage, so it took the self-description property measured on all five (e426) as the defensible ground.
