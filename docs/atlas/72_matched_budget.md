# 72. The matched-budget comparison: fields, SAE directions and random directions as a neuron's input, and the fields conditional on an SAE (S164)

**Question.** The note's reviewer asked for the comparison the fields' claim needs: the same number of directions for fields, SAE directions and random directions as a neuron's input, chosen on held-out text; the off-class suppression each reproduces; and whether the fields add anything once an SAE reconstruction is given. e723 answers it on the readers' turf with two dictionaries, e724 on the state.

**Established.**
- The matched-budget comparison on the readers' turf: beside sixteen wires, sixteen field directions leave 0.24 to 0.46 of the ablation damage at the firings against 0.23 to 0.42 for sixteen SAE directions and 0.25 to 0.51 for random, at the fields' full dimension 0.14 to 0.18 against 0.09 to 0.16 and 0.25 to 0.30; the SAE's full reconstruction leaves 0.10 to 0.21 and 0.06 to 0.11 with its error projected on the fields; off the class sixteen field directions reproduce the crowd's net sum at a correlation of 0.30 to 0.39 against 0.15 to 0.20 for sixteen SAE directions: reading: at the same number of directions the fields do not beat an SAE's directions at a neuron's firings; they beat them on the suppression itself at small budgets, and both beat random. Beside sixteen wires of GPT-2 small blocks 6 and 9, sixteen field directions chosen on held-out text leave 0.24 and 0.46 of the ablation damage at the firings against 0.23 to 0.31 and 0.41 to 0.42 for sixteen directions of jbloom's L1 and OpenAI's TopK dictionaries and 0.25 and 0.51 for random; at sixty-four, 0.18 and 0.25 against 0.23 to 0.25 and 0.25 to 0.26, random 0.24 and 0.43; at the fields' full dimension (192 and 288), 0.14 and 0.18 against 0.15 to 0.16 and 0.09 to 0.12, random 0.25 and 0.30. Over all positions, where the brake lives, sixty-four field directions leave 0.46 to 0.48 against 0.58 to 0.68 for sixty-four SAE directions, and the fields reproduce the crowd's off-class net sum at a correlation of 0.30 to 0.39 with sixteen directions and 0.52 to 0.60 with sixty-four, against 0.15 to 0.20 and 0.38 for the SAE's directions and 0.12 and 0.23 to 0.27 for random; at the full dimension the two tie (0.66 to 0.67 against 0.65 to 0.72). The SAE's own coefficients on sixteen latents chosen per position (0.13 to 0.27 at the firings) beat every fixed sixteen-direction subspace, with the block's own attention exact in that condition and projected in the others; and the SAE's error projected on the fields improves its full reconstruction at the firings (0.10 to 0.21 to 0.06 to 0.11), as does the projection on random directions by less (to 0.07 to 0.13) and on the SAE's own unused directions by more (to 0.04 to 0.05). Measured at a matched budget, the fields' claim is the brake's: they are the compact carrier of the off-class suppression, not a better dictionary for the firings. (e723).
- The fields conditional on an SAE reconstruction of a state: sixteen field atoms add 0.005 to 0.006 of the mean-ablation KL to the SAE's full reconstruction (0.976 to 0.989), sixteen native rows 0.006 to 0.012, sixteen of the SAE's own directions 0.005 to 0.011 and sixteen random directions 0.001 to 0.003, with intervention fidelity 0.93 to 0.97 against 0.83 to 0.88: reading: conditional on an SAE's full reconstruction of a state, the fields add nothing to its effect and something to its response. At GPT-2 small blocks 5 and 8 with jbloom's L1 and OpenAI's TopK dictionaries the SAE's full reconstruction recovers 0.976 to 0.989 of the mean-ablation KL; sixteen more atoms chosen by pursuit on what it leaves add 0.002 to 0.006 from the field directions, 0.006 to 0.012 from the native rows, 0.005 to 0.011 from the SAE's own directions and 0.001 to 0.003 from random directions. Under intervention the SAE's reconstruction tracks a fixed perturbation at 0.83 to 0.88 and with sixteen field atoms at 0.93 to 0.97, as high as with sixteen rows (0.91 to 0.97) and higher than with sixteen of the SAE's own directions (0.89 to 0.95) or random atoms (0.85 to 0.89). What the SAE leaves of a state is worth little to the output and something to an intervention, and sixteen field atoms recover that part as well as sixteen native rows do. (e724).
