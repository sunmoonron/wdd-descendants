# 56. Lottery or recruitment: the ticket at initialisation (S127)

**Question.** The small-initialisation theory predicts that the neuron which comes to carry a feature is the one already most aligned with it at initialisation. In real pretraining, is the row recruited for a class the one that was nearest at step 0, or is it chosen by the states during training?

**Established.**
- At step 0, before any training, the row that will be recruited for a class is in the top fifth of rows by alignment with the direction its class will have, a weak ticket rather than the nearest row: its median normalised rank by cosine with the final class direction is 0.15 to 0.21 across the three seeds (in the top 100 of 21504 rows for 0.05 to 0.08; a random row 0.51, the row against another class's direction 0.40), and by activation selectivity for its future class positions 0.33 to 0.38 (top 100 for 0.03 to 0.07); the rank is 0.12 by step 512 and 0.03 at step 1000, the lineage (top tenth and staying) is visible by step 512 for 0.43 of the eventual words (selectivity 0.53) and the step-0 rank does not set the recruitment time (Spearman 0.07) (e632).
- The ticket survives the controls at a weaker level: with the row's own write subtracted from the class states the step-0 rank is 0.32 against the final direction and 0.30 against the direction two checkpoints before recruitment (seeds 1 and 2: 0.32 / 0.32, 0.33 / 0.30); for classes defined by another seed's rows and carried over by position, the speaker found in seeds 1 and 2 has a step-0 rank of 0.12 and 0.23 in its own seed (89 and 105 classes; random 0.52), where the circularity of a class defined by the row itself cannot arise, and seed 0 against seed 1's classes 0.18; the recruited rows rotate from their step-0 direction as far as other rows do (cosine with the step-0 direction at recruitment 0.20 against 0.19 for all rows), so the initial direction is a small part of the final one and the ticket is a bias, not the race (e632).
- Two checkpoints before recruitment the recruit's read-weight norm beats its ten nearest competitors' median for 0.49 of words and its write norm for 0.65 (seeds 1 and 2: 0.48 / 0.53, 0.38 / 0.52), chance being 0.5, so the read-write balance of Wang 2026 does not pick the winner among the near rows here; given the class, two checkpoints before, the recruit is in the top ten of the non-word rows by mean class ratio for 0.84 (median rank 1), by usage for 0.31 (median 111), by the autoencoder's decoder cosine for 0.02 and by the class-side score for 0.04: the identification task is the class ratio's alone (e632).
- Reading: real pretraining sits between the theory's lottery and pure recruitment: initialisation biases which row is recruited (the recruit is in the top fifth by alignment with its future class direction before training, top 100 of 21504 for about one in twenty, in its own seed and for classes defined by another seed's rows, and in the top third once its own write is subtracted), but it is not the nearest row at initialisation, its initial rank does not set when it is recruited, half of the lineages are not yet visible at step 512, and the rows rotate as far from their initial direction as any row; the states choose among the rows that initialisation has shortlisted, and the class-side forecast of sessions 108 and 125 reads that choice as it is made; given the class, the class ratio names the recruit at a median rank of 1 among the non-word rows two checkpoints before, where usage puts it at a rank of 111 to 194: the identification task is the geometry's.
