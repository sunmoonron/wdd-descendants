# 66. The field-augmented dictionary (S158)

**Question.** WDD read a state as native rows over the floor plus a crowd; sessions 156 and 157 found the crowd to be a learned brake living in a sixteen-direction field per block. If the field is a second kind of atom, are its directions native to the weights, does rows plus field close the state's residual and the readers' error, do its directions read like neurons, and does the brake form the same way in a gated model?

**Established.**
- The block's Jacobian at the mean state points at the field without carrying it: its directions overlap the weak write's sixteen by 0.30 to 0.40 across 8 models, the true weak write on its sixty-four recovers 0.31 to 0.87 of the zeroing cost against 0.66 to 0.96 on the field's own sixteen, and the Taylor prediction from the weights recovers -2.72 to 0.70 against 0.56 to 0.96 for the fitted bypass, which holds out of domain (0.32 to 0.96 on code, 0.20 to 0.94 on EuroParl): reading: the block's own linearisation points at the field without carrying it: the Jacobian of the MLP at the mean normalised input has sixteen leading directions that overlap the weak write's sixteen by 0.30 to 0.40 in every model (weighting the neurons by their weak share changes nothing, since every neuron is weak at three quarters of positions by construction), and the true weak write projected on the Jacobian's sixty-four directions recovers 0.31 to 0.87 of the zeroing cost against 0.66 to 0.96 on the field's own sixteen; the first-order Taylor prediction of the weak write from the weights, the mean state and the weak shares, with no regression, recovers 0.29 to 0.70 in the Pythia models and GPT-2 large, makes things worse than zeroing in GPT-2 medium (-0.27) and in every gated model (-1.6 to -2.7), because a block's weak regime is not its tangent at the mean; the fitted rank-16 bypass, frozen after wikitext, keeps 0.65 to 0.97 of its recovery on GitHub code, ArXiv, EuroParl and StackExchange text in the GELU and GPT-2 models and 0.20 to 0.64 in the gated ones; so the atoms are native in the sense that the weights' linearisation finds a third of their subspace, and a property of the model on its input distribution in the sense that only a fit on activations finds the rest, and that fit travels across domains (e702).
- Rows plus field: sixteen wires plus the attention units plus the field's 128 directions bring a neuron's ablation ratio to 0.18 to 1.07 at its firings and 0.60 to 3.27 over all positions (sixteen wires alone 0.41 to 1.95 and 0.83 to 7.00), the field beating random subspaces of its dimension by 0.16 to 0.72; on the state's energy the field is 0.36 to 0.59 against the stream's own directions' 0.25 to 0.44: reading: the field is what the readers need and not where the state's energy is: at a block's input sixteen OMP rows over the floor leave 0.46 to 0.71 of the centred state's energy and remove every row over the floor, and the field then removes no more energy than a random subspace of its dimension (0.26 to 0.62 against 0.27 to 0.64 for sixteen per block, 0.36 to 0.59 against 0.37 to 0.62 for the union's 128, the stream's own principal directions 0.25 to 0.44), so WDD's residual after the rows is isotropic to the floor's eye and the field is not a dense part of it; but for a neuron's input the same field is specific: sixteen wires plus the field bring the ablation ratio at the firings from 0.41 to 1.95 to 0.29 to 0.93, a random subspace of the same dimension to 0.39 to 1.54 and the stream's directions to 0.31 to 2.03, and adding the attention units and the embedding as exact wires changes nothing (0.39 to 2.24), so the missing half of a pruned graph is in the MLP crowd's field and nowhere else; the union's 128 directions do no better than sixteen per block, and the best graph, wires with attention and the field, reaches 0.18 to 1.07 at the firings, under a half in six blocks of eight and under a quarter in one (e703).
- The field's directions read like neurons: their downstream causal response is as concentrated as a strong neuron's (0.16 to 0.27 against 0.15 to 0.23 in the top 1 percent) and as consistent in sign (0.78 to 0.97), their classes more lexical than neurons' (0.09 to 0.20 against 0.07 to 0.08 one-token share), their token images 0.07 to 0.09 concentrated: reading: the field's directions read like neurons: pushed by one standard deviation of its coordinate, a direction changes the next three blocks' neurons with a response as concentrated as a strong neuron's (0.16 to 0.27 of the energy in the top 1 percent of downstream neurons against 0.15 to 0.23 for the neurons, with 400 to 2000 effective responders in both), as consistent in sign across positions (0.78 to 0.97 against 0.79 to 0.95), with a token image as concentrated through the unembedding (0.07 to 0.09 against 0.06 to 0.09), and with a class of its own that is more lexical than a neuron's (one-token share 0.09 to 0.20 against 0.07 to 0.08, individual directions 0.34 to 0.51) and signed (half of the largest coordinates positive); the first direction, the mean weak write, has 290 to 2700 effective readers and in SmolLM2 a token image of punctuation and spaces; so the collective atoms are not an error term but units with the same kind of downstream job as neurons, each read by a few hundred to two thousand of them (e704).
- In a gated model the brake forms late: at OLMo-1B's blocks 12, 4, 8 the crowd's net sum off the class is -0.27 to -0.05 of the sixteen wires' at step 1000 and -0.69 to -0.23 in the final model, while at the core the crowd starts as the drive (1.14 to 1.88 of the wires') and the wires take over (0.18 to 0.37 to 0.52 to 0.76 of the net drive) (e701: reading: in a gated model the brake forms late: at OLMo-1B's blocks 4, 8 and 12 the crowd's net sum off the class is -0.05 to -0.27 of the sixteen wires' at step 1000 and stays between -0.31 and +0.01 through step 16000 (one percent of training, where Pythia-160m at the same fraction had already flipped sign), then reaches -0.23, -0.56 and -0.69 in the final model; at the core the crowd starts as the drive itself (1.1 to 1.9 times the wires') and the wires take it over by the end (their net share rising from 0.18 to 0.37 to 0.52 to 0.76), as in Pythia; so the sparsification of the drive is common to both families and the brake's timing is not, consistent with e686's split by nonlinearity and with the weaker suppression e695 found in Qwen's shallow block (e701o).
