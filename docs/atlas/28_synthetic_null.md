# 28. The synthetic null: what needs no training, which ingredient makes which statistic, and what the coupling is (S87-S89)

**Question.** Sessions 83-86 reduced the native vocabulary's distinction to persistent, individually oriented extreme axes with a wide basin set by the margin. How much of the whole phenomenology (birth, breadth before the maximum, the fast crossing, turnover, retention, the increments' memory) appears in a system with no training at all; which ingredient of a row's motion makes which statistic; and is the native excess a property of the rows, of their trajectories, or of the trajectories' relation to the cloud?

**Established.**
- Most of it needs no training: 8192 unit rows diffusing on the sphere against a fixed Gaussian cloud with the real block-12 covariance show a prospective criterion (0.80 at one interval, 0.75 at four), breadth rising before the maximum (12 against 6 two intervals before entry), turnover and retention (0.32); they lack words above the floor (S 1.00 at entry), a crossing to a plateau, the increments' memory and the native retention; a weak pull along the cloud's covariance supplies the levels (AUC 0.88, retention 0.52, S rising to 1.21 two intervals after entry) but not the memory (e536).
- Which ingredient makes which statistic (six synthetic worlds, same step size): isotropic diffusion gives the generic criterion (0.73), breadth before the maximum, turnover and retention 0.31; memory alone (lag-1 0.26, no relation to the cloud) nothing more (0.69, 0.24); covariance-shaped noise less (0.18); the pull along the covariance the native excess (0.88, 0.52, S rising through the floor to 1.25); memory with the pull the same plus the lag correlation (0.24). Pythia's own increment sequences given to other rows keep the phenomenology (0.88, 0.47) and collapse when their coherence is destroyed (0.70, 0.10) (e537).
- The native fifth is the alignment of the rows' motion with the contemporaneous cloud: Pythia's real rows with their sequences intact, against the same cloud turned by one fixed rotation, give the criterion 0.72 and retention 0.13 with the words at the floor (real cloud 0.91, 0.50, plateau 1.27); against a Gaussian cloud with each checkpoint's covariance they keep most of it (0.88, 0.41, a crossing to 1.08): the rows move into the cloud's covariance directions, the cloud's positions and tails adding the plateau's height and a tenth of retention (e538).
- The pull's sign is necessary (reversed: 0.59, 0.08), and a pull fading toward a target margin gives a slow approach (1.01, 1.07, 1.11 at target 1.25), not Pythia's jump from 1.11 to 1.22 and stop; reversing time keeps most of the excess (0.86, 0.45), the covariance directions being stable through training, and the radial part of the motion carries the rows' growth and none of the statistics (e538).

## How the results flow

- `e531 → e536`. The generic four fifths of the criterion reproduced with no training at all.
- `e536 → e537`. The null as a tool: the factorial names the ingredient (a coherent motion directed into the cloud), and the replay finds it in Pythia's own increment sequences.
- `e537 → e538`. The coupling: the sequences' motion is aimed at the cloud they face (a turned cloud loses the fifth, a Gaussian cloud with the same covariance keeps most of it); the sign is necessary; a fading pull is not the plateau; and the reversed-time replay points at the exits, read in area 29 (e538b, e540).
