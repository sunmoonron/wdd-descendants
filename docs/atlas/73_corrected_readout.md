# 73. The corrected readout: the core results re-measured after the double-norm audit (S165)

**Question.** An audit of the helper every script since e680 shares found that its log-probability function applied the model's final norm to a hidden state the base model had already normalised, so every KL from sessions 155 to 164 was measured through a doubly-normalised unembedding. Which of the record's KL-based claims survive once the norm is applied once?

**Established.**
- The corrected readout: with the final norm applied once, the strongest-quarter cost is 0.32 to 8.06 nats across 12 models (recorded 0.24 to 8.68), the brake's off-class share 0.40 to 0.88 (recorded 0.52 to 0.88) with the excitatory runaway at 4 to 503 times the ablation damage, both fields at the firings 0.13 to 0.52 against random 0.27 to 1.14, and a trained dictionary's top sixteen at 0.91 to 0.99 against 0.77 to 0.95 for sixteen rows: reading: the record's two broadest claims survive the corrected readout, its weak case gets weaker, and the intervention fidelities of the dictionary comparisons were the numbers the distortion had inflated most. The cost of keeping only the strongest quarter of each neuron's activations is 0.32 to 8.06 nats across twelve models against 0.24 to 8.68 recorded, with the mean replacement 0.14 to 0.98 against 0.07 to 2.31: GPT-2 small rises from 0.24 to 2.10 and GPT-2 medium from 0.95 to 3.96, SmolLM2-135M falls from 2.22 to 0.93 and Qwen2.5-7B from 1.57 to 0.32, and OLMo-1B, whose final norm has no gain, does not move. The brake's off-class share is 0.40 to 0.88 against 0.52 to 0.88, unchanged to two decimals at Pythia-410m, Qwen2.5-0.5B and SmolLM2 and down to 0.40 and 0.46 at GPT-2 medium, whose readout the doubling had distorted most; the excitatory runaway is 4 to 503 times the ablation damage (6 to 561 recorded) at every block, the signed wires beat the sixteen by magnitude at the firings in five blocks of eight (six recorded), and the Pile numbers follow the corrected wikitext ones within 0.03. Both fields bring the sixteen wires at the firings to 0.13 to 0.52 (0.12 to 0.53) against 0.27 to 1.14 for a random subspace. On a state the recovered KLs are unchanged, a trained dictionary's top sixteen at 0.91 to 0.99 against 0.77 to 0.95 for sixteen rows, and the fields add 0.00 to 0.01; the intervention fidelities all fall, the rows' from 0.65 to 0.89 to 0.50 to 0.81 and pursuit over OpenAI's directions from 0.34 to 0.49 to 0.06 to 0.30, so the rows' advantage over the L1 encoder under intervention is now one block of two. At a matched budget sixteen field directions leave 0.23 and 0.38 at the firings against 0.30 and 0.33 to 0.36 for sixteen SAE directions and 0.33 and 0.40 for random. The measurement was wrong by a model-dependent amount; the orderings that carried the record's claims mostly survived it, and the ones that did not were the fragile ones, GPT-2 medium's off-class share and the eight-perturbation fidelities.
