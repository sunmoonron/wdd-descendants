# 03. Where provenance survives: increments, depth, targets, and the function of what is read

**Question.** Where does a write's provenance stay readable between its block and the state, scored against what target, and does what is read carry function?
**Established.** Provenance is lost when writes accumulate, not when a block writes them: top-1 and top-8 true writes are read at 0.85-1.00 and 0.67-0.97 from the block's increment but 0.62-0.83 and 0.40-0.59 from the state (e131, dual reading); a hook-free increment pipeline lifts matched-budget top-1 identification from 0.46-0.70 to 0.78-1.00 at a cost in reconstruction (e02, e44). Part of the ceiling was the target: the largest surviving write is read at 0.76-0.94 by the dual reading (e119; e47's target was near-circular). Readability falls with depth under OMP and the dual alike as prominence falls (e49, e198), by amplification of a shared direction in GPT-2 and SmolLM2 and by erasure in Pythia and OLMo (e21, e76). An identified atom names who wrote a direction, not what caused it (e211); identified writes are more functional per unit energy than unidentified ones in four of five models (e165) but carry at most 1% of first-order loss attribution (e156).

## How the results flow

- `e01 → e21 → e76 → e222`: in GPT-2 and SmolLM2 reading decays while the direction is amplified, not erased (KILLED), and WDD's own estimate calls those writes gone; e222 (S4) recasts it as decorrelation.
- `e22 → e47 → e119 → e128`: GPT-2's late collapse is a target problem (late-born largest writes cancelled in their block); e47's fix was near-circular (audit), so e119 scored the largest surviving write and e128 took it through depth.
- `e49 → e198 → e211`: the dual reading decays too (a state property); prominence tracks readability across birth block and level, and reading follows prominence, not the write's causal share (H4a killed).
- `e02 → e44 → e131 → e147 → e167`: the increment reading became a matched-budget pipeline (audit caveats leave the recall gains standing); e131 and e147 put the loss at accumulation (4/5); e167 predicts the curve without fitting where e114's random floor failed.
- `e11 → e77 → e168`: after the v1 artifact, attention is irrelevant to MLP reading except in Pythia; GPT-2 is competition-limited, the others cancellation-limited; in three models early increments reinforce later writes, so peeling them hurts.
- `e44 → e144 → e159`, `e44 → e166`: increment nominations raise the state support's real-write share to 0.74-0.96 at small function cost, but summed increment reconstructions never match global OMP's spliced loss (e105).
- `e156 → e152 → e160 → e165`: loss sensitivity and reading are nearly disjoint per write, yet the identified part is more functional per unit energy; OLMo narrowed e165 to 4/5 (KILLED).
- `e25 → e436`: trained ledgers are far sparser than at init; e436 (S44) finds Pythia's writes sparsest mid-training and denser by the end.
