# 26. The drift's cause: the gradient at scale, the input side, the curvature, the common flow, the bound (S79-S82)

**Question.** e524b found that the rows near the floor drift in a consistent direction for thousands of steps, the entrants' drift the more directed, and e522b that 65k tokens cannot estimate a row's gradient. What gives the drift its direction: the row's own loss gradient once it is estimated at training-batch scale, raw or Adam-whitened; the network's own geometry at the neuron's active positions; or the flatness of the loss along the motion?

**Established.**
- The drift is not the row's mean gradient at the time: estimated twice on 1.5M tokens of the training distribution, a row's gradient agrees with itself at cosine 0.14/0.11/0.08 (steps 4000/8000/12000; less for neurons that fire more, Spearman -0.2 to -0.4), the thousand-step update follows it at 0.012-0.013 (positive for 63-65% of rows; at most 0.04 corrected for reliability), the Adam-whitened direction is the same in consistency and following (the gradient noise isotropic across a row's coordinates, per-coordinate signal-to-noise 0.07-0.08), the update's size is unrelated to the gradient's size, and the entrants' gradients are no more consistent (AUC 0.47-0.59), no better aimed at the state at their best position (cosine 0.00) and no more followed than matched rows' (e525, e525b).
- A small persistent component of an entrant's thousand-step motion is what its neuron sees when it fires: cosine 0.05-0.10 with the mean centred state at its active positions (own write removed), 0.04-0.09 with the block-12 state there, 0.05-0.09 with the state at its best position (typical rows about 0.00; AUC against matched 0.54-0.71), not with the co-active writes (0.00-0.02); the five origin-time targets explain 2-4% of the increment's energy (0.4% for random directions), rising to 5-9% over four thousand steps; the bulk is growth along the row (0.14-0.32) and motion the origin's geometry does not give (e526).
- The typical row's write drifts away from its read direction (cosine -0.10/-0.07/-0.05 per thousand steps at origins 4000/8000/12000, positive for 15-24% of rows); the entrants' and the words' do not (e526).
- The motion has long memory: the cosine between unit thousand-step increments two to six thousand steps apart is 0.11-0.17 for entrants and 0.03-0.09 for typical rows, with a mean-reverting dip at lag one (typical rows -0.04 to -0.13 late in training) (e526).
- The drift is not in flat directions: the curvature of the loss in the row's coordinates along the thousand-step update is 1.13-1.15 times that along a random direction (flatter than random for 0.20-0.24 of rows; entrants no flatter than matched, AUC 0.40-0.45), the negative gradient's direction 3.80-4.68 times, the row's own direction 1.23-1.45 times (e527).
- The row set does not move as one: the best single linear map over all 53,248 rows (dW = W M) carries 0.06-0.10 of the increments' energy at 1000-5000 and 0.02-0.03 by 15000 (permutation null -0.02), is mostly symmetric growth early (0.16-0.20 per thousand steps at 1000-3000) and increasingly rotation late, favours the cloud's top-32 components only in the first interval (1.45 times random, equal or below afterwards), persists (maps one to six intervals apart at cosine 0.42-0.48) and carries four tenths of the increments' memory; applied to the origin's rows it reproduces 0.11-0.26 of the entrants' rise of S before step 5000 and none after, and reads entry at chance from 5000 on (e528).
- Separated by timescale, the eight-thousand-step displacement (mostly drift) is 1.09-1.18 times as curved as a random direction (about as curved as a random direction), the thousand-step update 1.15-1.18, the oscillation axis (the difference of consecutive increments) 1.15-1.17; entrants and matched rows alike (e529).
- The bound: fitted on half the rows and scored on the other half, the best predictor of a row's eight-thousand-step displacement from everything at the origin explains 0.049-0.072 of its energy, all of it growth along the row and repulsion from the read direction; the context targets without the row 0.001-0.001; the non-radial part at 0.015 or less; the destination's geometry no better (0.035-0.066); entrants no more predictable than matched rows; the size of the motion predictable at R2 0.71-0.76 from the neuron's activation statistics (e530).

## How the results flow

- `e524b → e525, e525b`. The drift's cause tried on the instantaneous gradient at training-batch scale, raw and whitened: null; the drift is a slow variable of the trajectory.
- `e525-e529 → e530`. The bound instead of another mechanism: the direction of the motion is predicted at a few percent by everything in hand, at either end of the interval; the size at three quarters. The line closes here.
- `e526, e527 → e528, e529`. The program's own turn: the row set as a whole (a common flow: a tenth, persistent, not the entrants) and the drift separated from the oscillation by timescale (the eight-thousand-step displacement 1.09-1.18 times as curved as random, the oscillation axis 1.15-1.17).
- `e525b → e526, e527`. The two remaining handles: the input side gives a few percent of the motion, persistent and accumulating; the curvature gives nothing, the motion being as curved as a random direction.
