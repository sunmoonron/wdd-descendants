# WDD tracks the recruitment and changing responsibility of model parameters for persistent representation-space structure

*Draft of the write-up, session 109. The claims below are each tied to the experiments that established them; the atlas (`ATLAS.md`) and the findings log carry the numbers in full. Machine addresses and credentials are not part of this record.*

## Abstract

Weight-Dictionary Decomposition (WDD) decomposes a transformer's residual-stream state into the model's own write vectors, the rows of its MLP down-projections, its attention heads' output bases and its embeddings, with exact parameter provenance: it reports which of the model's own directions a state is built along, and how strongly. Over 580 experiments on Pythia-160m and 410m, OLMo-1B, GPT-2, Mamba-130m and 370m, two vision transformers and a grokking toy, the "native words" the instrument finds, rows that the states make extreme at recurring classes of context, turned out to be three objects that the word had conflated. The **class** is a partition of contexts that the corpus imposes: it is predictable from surface features of the text, it is shared by independently trained models across seeds, scales, architectures and families, and it exists in the state cloud from the first checkpoint, before any parameter speaks it. The **direction** along which a class is spoken is the model's own and carries the class's downstream effect. The **row** is the direction's implementation: it is recruited to a class it is already nearest to, predictably from the states alone thousands of steps ahead; it is produced by a coalition of thousands of small writes that any dictionary can find; it can be transplanted as a label but not as computation; and it can be replaced. WDD does not find features that learned dictionaries cannot, and it does not predict generalisation better than the classic signals. What it supplies for free, because its dictionary is the model's parameterisation, is the parameter side of one process: which parameter becomes responsible for a persistent piece of representation-space structure, and when.

## 1. The falsifiable sequence

The record supports one sequence, each step of which was open to refutation and several of which were refuted in their first form.

1. **A persistent structure appears in the states before any parameter carries it.** Every one of 241 eventual words' classes coheres in the block-12 cloud of Pythia-410m at step 1000, measured with no row involved (e584); the class's own direction, the unit mean of its states, is at cosine 0.85 with its step-16000 direction at step 4000 and 0.86 at the final model (step 143000), when the row that speaks it at 16000 is the speaker for 6% and 38% of classes respectively (e585). The partition is the corpus's: the token, the previous token, the position and four flags predict a word's context set at AUC 0.85 (e574); Pythia-160m runs with independent initialisation and data order twin by context sets at 0.34-0.38 against a null of 0.04, and Pythia-410m twins with Mamba-130m at 0.46 and with OLMo-1B at 0.41 by character spans across family, tokenizer and corpus (e557, e566).

2. **A particular parameter becomes aligned with it, and the aligned one is recruited.** Two checkpoints before a row is recruited it is already the first of 53,248 rows by its projection ratio over the class for 52% of eventual words and in the top ten for 84%; eight checkpoints before, 62% (e587). Forecast honestly, with the classes at time t taken from row-free clusters of the states and no future in their definition, the recruits are picked two checkpoints ahead at AUC 0.93 on both Pythia-410m and OLMo-1B (e588). Recruitment is not a performance event: aligned on it the class's loss falls as its token-matched controls do (difference -0.0102 [-0.0292, +0.0116] nats) and the row's direction is already load-bearing at the class two checkpoints before (0.0047 nats removed, against 0.0109 two after) (e591). The row turns toward directions where the cloud already has variance (1.037 times the old variance against 0.965 for a random turn of the same size) and the cloud does not grow along the row (e571); at one-checkpoint resolution the rise of an entrant is the row's part (+0.045) more than the cloud's (+0.032), and over eight thousand steps the row's part is five times the cloud's (e558b). The learning signal behind the turn is small per batch and consistent: two checkpoints before recruitment the eventual row's gradient step points toward its class's direction at mean cosine +0.020 (four standard errors above zero; matched non-recruits and random rows at zero), and the class's positions supply 22% of the row's gradient at 0.6% of the positions, against 9% for matched non-recruits (e589).

3. **Recruitment is one row, after a gap, and the coalition precedes it.** A class has one speaker row over sixteen checkpoints at the median, after a gap of eight checkpoints during which no row is over the floor at half of it; the 187 handoffs that do occur among 253 classes pass through overlaps in 183 cases (e585). The coalition of producers coheres along the eventual row five checkpoints before recruitment for 84% of the words that reach it (e584), as it did at coarser resolution (e546).

4. **Distributed writes implement the direction, and either dictionary can see them.** The extreme along a word is built by thousands of small writes: within-row coherence of the producer contributions 0.49 in Pythia-410m, 0.73 in OLMo-1B, 0.86 in Mamba-130m against 0.00 across rows (e545, e552, e570). An autoencoder's features on the same states have coalitions as coherent (0.69 against the words' 0.67) and as necessary (zeroing the top 128 producers drops a feature's activation at its class by 92%, a word's projection by 77%; random neurons under 1%), with different producers (Jaccard 0.07) (e581).

5. **The parameter can be replaced without destroying the structure.** Between checkpoints, a later neuron's write direction transplanted into an earlier model makes 54% of entrants words and moves the model's predictions at the class by 0.002 nats, away from the later model; with the 128 strongest producers as well, 93% become words and the predictions still move away; in the necessity direction the earlier producers disturb the later model's class by seven times more than elsewhere (e565, e577, e578). In the grokking toy, with the 32 eventual words frozen before recruitment the network still generalises perfectly with a vocabulary on other neurons, a third of the classes re-appearing on substitutes (e586). At scale, the removed speaker: Thirty words' write columns zeroed and frozen at step 8000 cost their classes +0.005 nats (the coalition builds the state; the row's own write is small), and 2.5M tokens of continued training on other Pile text produce no substitute speakers (0.17 of classes, against 0.23 with no training and 0.30 in the unfrozen continuation, all of them the classes' pre-existing second speakers) while raising the loss everywhere by about 0.1 nats, so the proxy degrades the model rather than continuing its training (e590). Replacement at scale is not reached within the proxy's means; the real test needs the training data order, and the record says so..

## 2. The five distinctions

- **Structure**: the partition of contexts into classes. Data- and training-induced, shared across models, surface-predictable, present from the first checkpoint. Not WDD's discovery: any clustering of the states or any learned dictionary sees it (e574, e581, e584).
- **Direction**: the persistent representational object. The class's own direction persists through training and outlives its row (e585); changing a row's direction changes the class's downstream effect, and within a model two words sharing a class have effects as alike as their directions (correlation 0.86, e573b); but the effect is a function of the direction, its producers and its readers together, not of the direction alone (e578).
- **Parameter**: the implementation that gets recruited. The nearest available row, predictable ahead of time (e587, e588); recruited by a small, consistent gradient drift driven by the class's own positions (e589); replaceable (e586, e590); a large row but not because it is large (norm-matched rows are used a fraction as often, sit under the floor and do not twin, e572).
- **Writers and coalition**: the mechanism implementing the direction. Thousands of producers, coherent across the class, necessary, present in Mamba as in the transformers (e545, e570, e581).
- **WDD**: the coordinate system for observing parameter responsibility. Its statistics at a fixed checkpoint are recoverable from a learned dictionary by attribution (e581); its detector of generalisation in the toy is beaten by the task's own Fourier signal (e580b); what it has that a learned dictionary lacks is the parameter behind each direction, which can be transplanted, frozen, followed through training and dated: a per-checkpoint autoencoder has a persistent feature for 31% of the classes and, at recruitment, a decoder at cosine 0.11 with the row (e584).

## 3. What WDD is not

- Not a better feature-discovery method: the producer-consumer decomposition belongs to any spoken direction attribution can reach (e581).
- Not a detector of corruption or of factual error: no single state announces an upstream corruption beyond two tokens, and the ledger does not see a factual error where the model's confidence does (e559, e560).
- Not an early predictor of generalisation: the native excess separates every generalising toy run from every memorising one at the step generalisation happens, not before, and below the task's own signal (e579, e580b).
- Not a finder of the true ontology: the class is the corpus's, the direction the model's, the row replaceable; "this neuron is the concept" is wrong (e573, e585, e586).
- Not a performance event: aligned on recruitment the class's loss falls as its token-matched controls do (difference -0.0102 [-0.0292, +0.0116] nats), and the row's direction removed from the class costs 0.0047 nats two checkpoints before recruitment and 0.0109 two after; what recruitment dates is the assignment of a parameter (e591).
- Not a pruning guide or a stitching anchor: S ranks the eventual words below average until generalisation and pruning helps grokking whatever is pruned (e593, e593c); twinned positions fit a stitch no better than random ones (e594).
- Not fixed by a better refit: the magnitude bias is the support's, removed by the oracle support and by nothing else (e596). ## 4. Methods, in brief

States after a block are centred and normalised; the dictionary is the model's own write rows (rows only, unit-normalised) for the training-dynamics results, the full native dictionary for the descriptive ones; OMP with sixteen atoms; a word is one of the 256 most used rows; the floor is an extreme-value bound calibrated on a rotated copy of the dictionary; S is a row's largest projection over the floor; a class is a word's over-the-floor positions; twins are best-Jaccard matches of context sets against a rotated-dictionary null; the coalition is the vector of every producer's contribution along a direction at the class's positions; the row-free class direction is the unit mean of a class's unit states; forecasts are leave-one-checkpoint-out; interventions are done on the parameters themselves. Every experiment was pre-registered with honest guesses and recorded whether or not it came out as guessed.

## 5. Limits

Twelve thousand positions of Pile text per checkpoint; sixteen checkpoints of one run at one depth for the developmental results, replicated on OLMo-1B where a cache existed; a small per-checkpoint autoencoder (4096 latents) as the longitudinal control, a longitudinally matched larger one still to be built; the toy's classes only partly fixed by its task; the row-free presence measure saturated and needing a surface-matched null; proxy continuations of training that cannot reproduce the real two billion tokens per interval. Session 110's nulls are the descriptive ones: the unlearning audit is one model, one domain and two bounded methods; the pruning result is the toy's; the superposition count depends on the clustering's grain. Session 111's audit is two models under 500M parameters, two domains, five methods at one learning rate each, and attacks of at most a hundred steps; 4-bit quantisation costs these models more than the unlearning did, so that attack is untested at this scale. Session 112's refusal audit is one 0.5B chat model with two weak prefix jailbreaks; its merging audit two domains; its compression audit one model. Session 113's rescue and refusal-direction results are one seed per model and domain; the 1B baseline is one seed. Session 114's transport maps are finite differences at one perturbation size on one model each. The TOFU bake-off is one 0.5B model, 12 usable conditions and a forget set that is not a domain (2 thresholded words, 32 ranked ones at 0.62 forget share); the in-context attack on the Pile is three sizes of one family and one prefix length. The held-out test is 255 conditions at one window split, with the held-out architecture at one size and the held-out methods at one implementation each. The controls and the per-word test are 160m only, the gradient methods only, at +2. The parameter-side directions are one model each: Qwen2.5-0.5B for the diffing and the edit, Pythia-160m for the fine-tune, 1500 steps. The catalogue is four attributes on two models of one family, with the side effects measured on Pile loss and the other three attributes only. The detection probe is one block of one model with forty edits; the free-text test is English pronouns only.

## 6. The sentence

The data gives the class and its direction; training recruits the nearest row to it, by a small drift the class's own positions drive; thousands of writers implement it; the row is replaceable. WDD is the instrument that follows the row.
